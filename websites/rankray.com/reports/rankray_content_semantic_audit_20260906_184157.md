## Executive Summary
Content audit reveals strong blog production but inconsistencies in service page depth and internal linking structure. Semantic SEO cluster architecture is partially implemented but requires refinement to avoid cannibalization and improve topical authority.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Service Page Content Depth
- Average word count across 54 service pages: 612 words (target: 1200-1500 for competitive niches).
- Only 12 pages exceed 1000 words.
- ACF field usage shows sparse population; many fields empty or containing placeholder text.

### 2. Blog Cluster Architecture
- Total blog posts: 148
- Clusters identified: General SEO (30), Technical SEO (11), Link Building (6), E-E-A-T (4), etc.
- 22 blog posts lack clear service page alignment (potential cannibalization risk).

### 3. Keyword Cannibalization Check
- 7 blog posts targeting same core keyword as service pages (e.g., "Technical SEO Services" service page vs blog "What is Technical SEO?").
- 4 location pages competing with service pages for geo-modified terms.

### 4. Internal Linking Matrix
- Average internal links per blog post: 5.2 (target: 8-12)
- Only 38% of contextual links use keyword-rich anchors.
- Footer and navigation links dominate link equity distribution.

### 5. GEO/AEO Readiness
- 0% of pages have FAQ schema (removed per policy).
- 15% of pages contain direct-answer format (H2 question + concise paragraph).
- Structured data for HowTo present on 8 blog posts.

### 6. Recommendations
1. Increase service page depth to 1200-1500 words with detailed service breakdowns.
2. Create pillar service pages for each vertical (Enterprise, Franchise, B2B) with supporting clusters.
3. Implement contextual internal linking plan: 1 link per paragraph, keyword-rich, varied anchors.
4. Add direct-answer sections to target GEO snippets.
5. Update internal linking SOP for all content creators.
