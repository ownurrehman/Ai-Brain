## What to Do After a Car Accident to Start Physiotherapy Recovery

A motor vehicle accident is a traumatic event, and the moments, hours, and days that follow are critical for your physical recovery. Knowing what to do after a car accident can help you protect your health, support your insurance claim, and set the stage for a faster return to normal life. At Tonic Physio in Milton, we guide car accident victims through every step of the recovery process with expert physiotherapy, personalized treatment plans, and direct insurance billing.

## Step One: Seek Medical Attention Immediately

Even if you feel fine after a car accident, you should be evaluated by a medical professional. Adrenaline masks pain, and some injuries like whiplash, internal bleeding, or concussion may not show symptoms for hours or even days. Go to the emergency room, an urgent care clinic, or your family doctor as soon as possible after the collision.

Keep all medical records, diagnoses, imaging results, and discharge instructions. These documents are essential for your physiotherapy assessment and any insurance or legal claims. They also help your physiotherapist understand the full scope of your injuries and build an appropriate treatment plan.

## Step Two: Report the Accident to Your Insurance Provider

Contact your auto insurance company within twenty-four hours of the accident to report the incident. In Ontario, your auto insurance policy includes accident benefits that cover physiotherapy and rehabilitation costs, regardless of who was at fault.

Your insurer will assign an accident benefits adjuster who manages your claim. Ask about your coverage for physiotherapy, massage therapy, and other rehabilitation services. Most auto insurance policies in Ontario provide a minimum of three thousand five hundred dollars for medical and rehabilitation benefits for minor injuries, with much higher limits for more serious conditions.

Tonic Physio works directly with all major insurance providers. We can help you understand your coverage, complete claim forms, and submit treatment plans on your behalf. Our team coordinates with your insurance adjuster to make sure your treatment is pre-approved and billed correctly.

## Step Three: Book a Physiotherapy Assessment Within Seventy-Two Hours

Early physiotherapy intervention is one of the most important factors in recovering fully after a car accident. We recommend booking your first assessment within twenty-four to seventy-two hours of the collision, even if your pain is mild. The sooner your therapist evaluates your condition, the sooner treatment can begin, and the lower your risk of developing chronic pain or permanent dysfunction.

At Tonic Physio, your initial motor vehicle accident assessment includes a full musculoskeletal evaluation, neurological screening, concussion assessment, and range of motion testing. We check your neck, back, shoulders, hips, knees, and any other areas affected by the impact. Your therapist documents everything, which supports both your recovery and your insurance claim.

## Step Four: Follow Your Treatment Plan Consistently

Your physiotherapist will create a personalized treatment plan based on your injuries, your health history, your work demands, and your recovery goals. This plan may include manual therapy, exercise therapy, massage therapy, shockwave therapy, or custom bracing depending on your condition.

Consistency is key. Attend all scheduled appointments, complete your home exercises, and follow your therapist's recommendations for activity modification. Skipping appointments or ignoring your exercises can slow recovery and extend the time you need treatment.

At Tonic Physio, we schedule appointments at times that fit your life including early mornings, evenings, and weekends where available.

## Step Five: Keep a Recovery Journal

Document your symptoms, activities, and progress after the accident. A recovery journal helps your physiotherapist adjust your treatment plan and provides valuable evidence for your insurance claim or legal case.

Note your daily pain levels on a scale of one to ten, any new symptoms, activities that worsen or improve your symptoms, sleep quality, mood changes, and any limitations at work or home. Bring this journal to every appointment.

## Step Six: Understand Your Legal Rights

If you were injured in a car accident caused by another driver, you may be entitled to additional compensation beyond your accident benefits. This can include damages for pain and suffering, lost income, future care costs, and out-of-pocket expenses.

Consult a personal injury lawyer if your injuries are moderate to severe, if your insurance company denies your claim, or if you are unsure about your rights. Tonic Physio works closely with personal injury lawyers in the Milton area and can provide the medical documentation needed to support your case.

## Step Seven: Address All Symptoms Not Just Pain

After a car accident, some symptoms are easy to overlook. Dizziness, headaches, difficulty concentrating, irritability, fatigue, and sleep disturbances can all be signs of concussion or vestibular dysfunction. Emotional symptoms like anxiety, depression, or post-traumatic stress are also common after collisions.

At Tonic Physio, our concussion management and vestibular rehabilitation programs address these symptoms directly. We do not just treat your pain; we treat your whole system to restore full function.

## Step Eight: Gradually Return to Normal Activity

Your physiotherapist will guide you through a progressive return to work, driving, exercise, and daily activities. Returning too quickly can re-injure healing tissues and set back your recovery. Returning too slowly can lead to deconditioning, stiffness, and loss of confidence.

Your treatment plan includes functional milestones that tell you when it is safe to increase activity. Trust the process and communicate openly with your therapist about how you feel during each stage.

## Step Nine: Use All Available Resources

Take advantage of all the rehabilitation resources available to you. At Tonic Physio, our multi-disciplinary team offers physiotherapy, registered massage therapy, manual osteopathy, shockwave therapy, TMJ treatment, and vestibular rehabilitation all in one location. Using a combination of therapies often produces faster and more complete results than relying on one approach alone.

## Step Ten: Plan for Long-Term Health

Recovery from a car accident does not end when your pain stops. Lasting recovery means restoring strength, mobility, confidence, and resilience. Continue your exercises after formal therapy ends, maintain good posture and ergonomics, manage stress, and stay active within healthy limits.

If you experience lingering symptoms months after the accident, return for a reassessment. Early intervention for flare-ups prevents minor issues from becoming chronic problems.

## Why Early Physiotherapy Matters After a Car Accident

Research consistently shows that early physiotherapy after motor vehicle accidents leads to better outcomes, shorter recovery times, and lower rates of chronic pain. Delaying treatment increases the risk of scar tissue formation, muscle imbalances, compensatory movement patterns, and long-term disability.
