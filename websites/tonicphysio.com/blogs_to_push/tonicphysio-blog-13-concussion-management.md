# Concussion Management and Physiotherapy: Recovery After Head Injury

A concussion is a mild traumatic brain injury that occurs when a blow to the head or body causes the brain to move rapidly within the skull. While many people recover within days or weeks, others experience persistent symptoms that interfere with daily life. At Tonic Physio in Milton, our concussion management program helps patients recover safely from post-concussion syndrome and return to work, school, and sport with confidence.

## Recognizing Concussion Symptoms

Concussion symptoms vary from person to person and may not appear immediately. Common symptoms include headache, dizziness, nausea, balance problems, sensitivity to light and sound, confusion, memory difficulty, fatigue, sleep disturbances, irritability, and difficulty concentrating.

If you suspect a concussion, seek medical evaluation immediately. Proper diagnosis rules out more serious injury and establishes the baseline needed for rehabilitation planning.

## Why Physiotherapy Is Important for Concussion Recovery

The brain needs both rest and gradual re-exposure to stimuli as it heals. Complete rest for extended periods can actually worsen outcomes by causing deconditioning, mood decline, and delayed recovery. Physiotherapy bridges the gap between acute rest and full return to activity.

Our concussion program at Tonic Physio addresses the systems most commonly affected after head injury. Vestibular rehabilitation treats dizziness, vertigo, and balance problems. Cervical spine therapy reduces headaches caused by neck muscle strain. Visual therapy improves eye tracking, focus, and coordination. Cardiovascular reconditioning gradually restores aerobic capacity without triggering symptom spikes. Cognitive pacing strategies help patients manage mental fatigue and return to school or work safely.

## The Tonic Physio Concussion Assessment

Your initial assessment includes a detailed history of the injury event, symptom inventory, neurological screen, vestibular and balance testing, cervical spine evaluation, visual system tests, and exertion tolerance testing using standardized protocols.

This assessment identifies which systems are affected and to what degree, which guides the customized treatment plan.

## Vestibular Rehabilitation After Concussion

The vestibular system controls balance and spatial orientation. After concussion, it can be disrupted, causing persistent dizziness and motion sensitivity. Our vestibular therapy includes habituation exercises that reduce sensitivity to movement, gaze stabilization training that improves visual focus during head motion, balance retraining on varied surfaces, and repositioning maneuvers for benign paroxysmal positional vertigo when present.

Most patients show improvement in vestibular symptoms within four to eight sessions.

## Cervicogenic Headache Treatment

Many post-concussion headaches originate in the neck rather than the brain. Whiplash forces during the impact strain cervical muscles, ligaments, and joints. Cervical physiotherapy including manual therapy, soft tissue release, and strengthening reduces these headaches significantly.

## Graduated Return to Activity

Return to sport, school, or work after concussion must follow a stepwise protocol. At Tonic Physio, we use internationally recognized return to play and return to learn guidelines. Each step introduces greater cognitive and physical demand. If symptoms increase at any stage, we pause progress until the patient is ready.

Our therapists coordinate with physicians, coaches, teachers, and employers to make sure every return is safe and well supported.

## When to Seek Concussion Physiotherapy

If your concussion symptoms persist beyond two weeks, physiotherapy is recommended. Early intervention prevents the downward spiral of rest, deconditioning, and symptom sensitivity that makes recovery more difficult. Most patients in our Milton program recover within six to twelve weeks with proper management.
