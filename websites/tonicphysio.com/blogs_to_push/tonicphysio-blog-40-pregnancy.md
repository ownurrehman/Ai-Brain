# Physiotherapy During Pregnancy: Managing Pain and Preparing for Birth

Pregnancy brings extraordinary changes to the body. Hormones loosen ligaments, weight increases, posture shifts, and the growing baby alters biomechanics in ways that can create significant discomfort. Many women assume they must simply endure back pain, pelvic pain, and other symptoms during pregnancy. At Tonic Physio in Milton, our prenatal physiotherapy program helps pregnant women manage pain, maintain fitness, and prepare for a healthy delivery.

## Common Pregnancy-Related Conditions

Pregnant women experience a range of musculoskeletal conditions.

Lower back pain caused by the forward shift in center of gravity and ligament laxity is one of the most common complaints. Pelvic girdle pain occurs at the front and back of the pelvis and can make walking, rolling in bed, and standing extremely painful. Sciatica and nerve compression occur as the growing uterus and ligaments press on the sciatic nerve. Diastasis recti is the separation of the abdominal muscles along the midline. Wrist and hand pain including carpal tunnel syndrome are caused by fluid retention and increased pressure on the wrist nerves. Rib and upper back pain develop as the rib cage expands. Calf cramps and swelling affect many women in the third trimester.

## Is Physiotherapy Safe During Pregnancy

Physiotherapy is safe during all stages of pregnancy when performed by a qualified and experienced therapist. At Tonic Physio, we use pregnancy-safe techniques and modify positions to avoid lying flat on your back during the second and third trimesters. We monitor your comfort and make adjustments as your body changes.

## How Prenatal Physiotherapy Helps

Our prenatal physiotherapy program addresses multiple goals.

Pain relief for the back, pelvis, and other areas is achieved through manual therapy, exercise, and ergonomic education. Pelvic floor preparation includes teaching you how to contract and relax these muscles, which supports labor and recovery. Core training focuses on deep abdominal muscles to support the spine while protecting the midline and preventing excessive diastasis recti. Posture and body mechanics education reduces strain during daily activities. Labor preparation includes breathing techniques, optimal positioning for labor, and perineal stretching to reduce injury risk during delivery.

## Pelvic Floor During Pregnancy

The pelvic floor supports the bladder, bowel, and uterus. During pregnancy, it carries increasing weight and undergoes significant stretch during delivery. Learning to activate and relax the pelvic floor properly during pregnancy is one of the best ways to reduce incontinence, prolapse, and tearing.

Our pelvic floor therapists provide prenatal assessments and teach effective techniques for pregnancy and postpartum.

## Postnatal Recovery

After delivery, the body needs to recover from profound changes. At Tonic Physio, our postnatal program starts as early as six weeks after delivery. We assess abdominal separation and provide exercises to restore core function. We screen pelvic floor strength and function. We address back and pelvic pain that may persist after delivery. We create progressive strength programs that prepare you for the physical demands of carrying, feeding, and caring for your baby.
