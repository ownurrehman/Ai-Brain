# Pediatric Physiotherapy: When Your Child Needs Help and What to Expect

As a parent, noticing that your child is not moving, developing, or recovering the way you expect can be concerning. You may wonder whether your concerns are valid and what type of help is available. Pediatric physiotherapy is a specialized area that helps children from birth through adolescence reach their movement potential, recover from injuries, and manage developmental or neurological conditions. At Tonic Physio in Milton, our therapists work with parents and children to create treatment plans that are effective, engaging, and age-appropriate.

## What Is Pediatric Physiotherapy

Pediatric physiotherapy focuses on the assessment and treatment of movement disorders and physical challenges in children. Unlike adult physiotherapy, which often centers on pain relief and recovery from injury, pediatric physiotherapy addresses developmental milestones, movement quality, neurological conditions, and congenital disorders.

Pediatric physiotherapists have specialized training in child development, pediatric neurological conditions, growth-related injuries, and family-centered care. They understand how to communicate with children of different ages and how to create exercises that feel like play rather than therapy.

## When Should You Consider Pediatric Physiotherapy

Parents and caregivers should consider pediatric physiotherapy when a baby shows signs of torticollis or head tilt. A toddler is significantly delayed in reaching movement milestones such as sitting, crawling, or walking. A child walks on their toes persistently. An older child complains of joint pain or muscle weakness. A child has a diagnosed neurological condition such as cerebral palsy or developmental coordination disorder.

If you have any concerns about your child's movement or development, consulting a pediatric physiotherapist is always appropriate. Early intervention leads to the best outcomes.

## Common Conditions Treated in Pediatric Physiotherapy

At Tonic Physio, our pediatric physiotherapy services address a wide range of conditions.

Developmental delay includes late achievement of gross motor milestones. We create milestone-based programs that help children catch up to their expected developmental timeline. Torticollis and plagiocephaly respond well to early intervention. We teach parents gentle stretching and positioning techniques that improve head shape and neck alignment.

Cerebral palsy requires ongoing management throughout childhood. We focus on maintaining mobility, managing spasticity, and supporting functional independence at every age. Toe walking is common in toddlers but requires treatment when it persists beyond age three. We address the underlying calf tightness and sensory patterns that maintain the habit.

Growth-related conditions such as Osgood-Schlatter disease, Sever disease, and patellofemoral pain are common in active children and adolescents. Treatment includes activity modification, strengthening, and education about safe return to sports.

Sports injuries in younger athletes differ from adult injuries because children are still growing. We treat sprains, strains, growth plate injuries, and overuse syndromes with protocols designed for developing bodies.

## What Happens During a Pediatric Assessment

Your child's first physiotherapy assessment at Tonic Physio is designed to be relaxed and informative. We start with a detailed history including birth details, developmental milestones, medical history, current symptoms, and family concerns.

Next, we observe your child at play. Watching how a child moves naturally tells us more than formal testing alone. We may assess range of motion, strength, balance, coordination, gait, and reflexes depending on your child's age and condition.

We then explain our findings in clear language and outline a treatment plan. Parental involvement is central to pediatric physiotherapy, so we teach you the home exercises and strategies that support your child's progress between sessions.

## Treatment Techniques in Pediatric Physiotherapy

Treatment varies based on the child's age, condition, and temperament.

Play-based exercises engage younger children while targeting specific motor skills. We use balls, balance beams, obstacles, and games to make therapy fun. Neuromuscular techniques improve muscle activation and coordination in children with neuromotor challenges.

Stretching and strengthening programs address muscle imbalances common in growing athletes and children with posture concerns. Gait training helps children with abnormal walking patterns develop more efficient and safe movement.

Hydrotherapy and sensory integration may be recommended for children who respond well to water-based or sensory-rich environments.

## How Parents Support Pediatric Therapy at Home

Parents are the most important part of a child's physiotherapy team. Completing prescribed home exercises daily, modifying activities to support therapeutic goals, creating a safe environment for movement exploration, maintaining consistent communication with the therapist, and celebrating small improvements all contribute to success.

At Tonic Physio, we provide written instructions, video demonstrations, and ongoing support to make home exercise programs manageable for busy families.

## When to Refer Your Child for Pediatric Physiotherapy

Your family doctor, pediatrician, or teacher may suggest pediatric physiotherapy. However, parents can also self-refer if they have concerns. If your child is struggling with movement, you do not need to wait for someone else to suggest therapy. Trust your instincts and book an assessment.
