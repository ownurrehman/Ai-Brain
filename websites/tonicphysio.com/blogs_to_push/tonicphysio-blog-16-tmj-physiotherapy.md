# TMJ Physiotherapy: Jaw Pain Relief Through Targeted Therapy

Temporomandibular joint disorder, often called TMJ dysfunction or TMD, is a condition that causes pain, clicking, locking, and limited movement in the jaw. It can affect your ability to eat, speak, and even sleep comfortably. Many people in Milton suffer from TMJ symptoms for years without knowing that physiotherapy is an effective treatment option. At Tonic Physio, our TMJ physiotherapy program targets the muscles, joints, and nervous structures that control jaw movement to provide lasting relief.

## What Causes TMJ Dysfunction

TMJ dysfunction can develop from several factors. Jaw clenching and teeth grinding place tremendous pressure on the joint and surrounding muscles, often during sleep. Poor posture, especially forward head position while working at a computer, creates tension in the neck and jaw. Direct trauma to the jaw or face can disrupt joint mechanics. Dental issues including malocclusion or missing teeth alter the way the jaw closes and moves. Stress increases muscle tension and bruxism throughout the day. Arthritis can affect the temporomandibular joint just like any other joint in the body.

At Tonic Physio, we assess all of these contributing factors during your initial evaluation.

## Symptoms That Respond to TMJ Physiotherapy

Common TMJ symptoms include jaw pain or tenderness, clicking or popping sounds when opening and closing the mouth, locking or limited opening, ear pain without infection, headaches originating around the temples, neck and shoulder tension, tooth sensitivity without dental decay, and pain when chewing or yawning.

If you experience any of these symptoms, a TMJ assessment at Tonic Physio can identify the root cause and start treatment immediately.

## What Happens During a TMJ Assessment

Your TMJ assessment at Tonic Physio includes a detailed history of your symptoms, habits, and any dental or medical history. We examine jaw range of motion, tracking, and any deviations that occur during opening and closing. We palpate the muscles of mastication and the cervical spine to identify trigger points and tension patterns. We assess cervical spine mobility since neck dysfunction commonly coexists with TMJ problems. We evaluate posture and breathing patterns that may be contributing to muscle overuse.

## Physiotherapy Treatment for TMJ

Treatment for TMJ dysfunction at Tonic Physio is comprehensive and non-invasive.

Manual therapy techniques include intra-oral and extra-oral massage to release the muscles that control jaw movement. These techniques are performed with gloves and are painless. Your therapist has advanced training in safe and effective intra-oral techniques. Joint mobilization restores normal gliding of the temporomandibular disc and reduces clicking and locking. Soft tissue release addresses the trigger points in the masseter, temporalis, and pterygoid muscles that contribute to pain and tension.

Exercise therapy focuses on controlled jaw range of motion exercises, stabilization exercises for the jaw opening muscles, cervical spine strengthening and flexibility, and posture correction drills. We also provide self-management strategies including heat and ice application, self-massage techniques, stress management advice, and diet modifications during flare-ups.

## The Role of Posture in TMJ Pain

Forward head posture is one of the most overlooked contributors to TMJ dysfunction. When your head sits forward of your shoulders, the muscles at the base of the skull and the jaw are under constant tension. At Tonic Physio, we address both the jaw and the neck as one integrated system, which leads to faster and more complete resolution of symptoms.

## How Long Does TMJ Treatment Take

Most patients experience noticeable improvement within four to six sessions. Complex cases involving disc displacement, arthritis, or chronic clenching may require eight to twelve sessions. We also coordinate with dentists and orthodontists when bite correction or dental splinting is needed.
