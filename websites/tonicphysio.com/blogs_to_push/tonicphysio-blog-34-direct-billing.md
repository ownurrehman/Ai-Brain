# Tonic Physio Direct Billing Guide: How Insurance Works at Our Clinic

Understanding how insurance and direct billing work is one of the most common questions patients have before starting physiotherapy. At Tonic Physio in Milton, we simplify the process by offering direct billing to most major insurance providers. This guide explains how our direct billing system works, what you need to bring, and what to expect when you visit us.

## What Is Direct Billing

Direct billing is a service where the physiotherapy clinic submits your treatment claim directly to your insurance provider instead of requiring you to pay upfront and seek reimbursement yourself. At Tonic Physio, we handle this process electronically for most insurers. This means you pay only the amount not covered by your plan at the time of your appointment, or in many cases, nothing at all.

Direct billing reduces your out-of-pocket expenses, eliminates the paperwork of filing claims, and allows you to track your insurance benefits while we manage the administrative side.

## Insurance Providers We Direct Bill

Tonic Physio provides direct billing to a wide range of insurance companies. Contact our clinic to confirm whether your specific provider is included.

Many popular extended health plans are supported, and we continue to expand our network based on patient demand.

## What You Need to Bring

To use direct billing at your first appointment, bring your insurance card or member identification number, the policy or group number, and the name of the policyholder if it is not yourself. If you were injured in a motor vehicle accident, bring your accident report, insurance company name, claim number, and adjuster contact information. For workplace injuries covered by WSIB, bring your claim number and any correspondence from the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board.

Having this information ready allows us to verify your coverage before your first treatment.

## How Direct Billing for MVA Claims Works

If your injury resulted from a motor vehicle accident, your treatment may be covered directly through your motor vehicle insurance. At Tonic Physio, we handle MVA direct billing for both minor and major claims.

Minor injury claims are typically covered under the statutory accident benefits schedule without a deductible. Major injury claims require additional documentation and approval. We assist you with the reporting process and communicate directly with your insurance adjuster.

Because MVA claims involve special reporting requirements, we recommend calling ahead before your first appointment so we can prepare your file in advance.

## How Direct Billing for WSIB Claims Works

Workplace injuries covered by the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board are billed directly through the WSIB system. We require your claim number and the contact information for your case manager. We submit treatment reports and updates through the WSIB portal after each session.

In some cases, WSIB authorization is required before treatment begins. If your claim is pending approval, we can discuss alternative payment options until authorization is received.

## What If Your Insurance Does Not Cover the Full Amount

If your insurance plan covers a portion of the treatment cost, you are responsible for paying the remaining balance at the time of your appointment. If your benefits are exhausted, you will be notified, and subsequent visits will require payment at the time of service.

We always explain your coverage and any gaps before your appointment so there are no surprises.

## Extended Health Benefits vs MVA vs WSIB

Extended health benefits cover physiotherapy, massage therapy, acupuncture, and other treatments through your employer or private insurance. These benefits usually have an annual dollar amount or visit limit. Motor vehicle accident benefits cover treatment for injuries sustained in a collision regardless of fault. Workplace safety and insurance board coverage is specific to workplace injuries and requires an approved claim.

At Tonic Physio, we help you understand which coverage applies to your situation.

## Our Billing Policies

We submit claims promptly after each session. We require accurate insurance information from you to prevent delays. We notify you if a claim is denied or requires additional information. We accommodate payment plan requests when insurance coverage is delayed or disputed. We provide receipts for all treatments, including those not covered by insurance, for tax purposes.
