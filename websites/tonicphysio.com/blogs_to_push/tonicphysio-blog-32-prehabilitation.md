# Prehabilitation: Preparing for Surgery with Physiotherapy

Prehabilitation, or prehab, is the practice of undergoing physiotherapy before surgery to improve outcomes and accelerate recovery. For patients preparing for joint replacement, ligament reconstruction, or other orthopedic procedures, prehab can make a significant difference in post-operative function and recovery time. At Tonic Physio in Milton, we offer prehabilitation programs for patients planning surgery in the near future.

## What Is Prehabilitation

Prehabilitation involves strengthening the muscles that will support the surgical area, restoring range of motion before it is compromised by surgery, improving cardiovascular fitness to tolerate anesthesia and immobilization, optimizing body weight to reduce joint stress, and educating the patient about post-operative expectations and exercises.

The goal is to enter surgery in the best possible physical condition so that recovery is faster, complications are fewer, and functional outcomes are better.

## Benefits of Prehabilitation

Research supports several benefits of prehabilitation. Stronger muscles before surgery make post-operative rehabilitation easier. Patients who have done prehab regain function faster and require less assistance. Better pre-operative range of motion leads to better post-operative mobility. Prehab reduces the length of hospital stay after joint replacement surgery. Patients who do prehab report less pain and better confidence after surgery. Reduced anxiety comes from knowing what to expect and being physically prepared.

## Who Should Consider Prehabilitation

Prehabilitation is particularly beneficial for patients preparing for total hip or knee replacement, ACL or ligament reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, spinal surgery, joint arthroscopy, or any procedure that will result in temporary immobilization or restricted activity.

Patients who are older, overweight, or deconditioned may benefit most because surgery represents a greater physiological challenge.

## What Prehabilitation Includes

Your prehabilitation program at Tonic Physio is tailored to your surgery, current fitness, and goals. We assess baseline strength, mobility, balance, and function. We design exercise programs to strengthen the muscles surrounding the surgical joint. We address any existing pain or limitations that might complicate post-operative recovery. We improve cardiovascular fitness through low-impact activity. We provide education on post-operative care, precautions, and early rehabilitation expectations. We coordinate with your surgeon to confirm timing, surgical approach, and any specific requirements.

## Prehabilitation Timeline

Ideally, prehabilitation begins four to eight weeks before surgery. This allows enough time to make meaningful improvements without causing fatigue or injury. Patients who start earlier can often achieve greater gains. Even two to four weeks of preparation is better than none.

At Tonic Physio, we adjust the program duration based on surgical timing and individual needs.

## Prehabilitation and Post-Operative Physiotherapy

Prehabilitation is the first phase of your complete surgical recovery. After surgery, your post-operative physiotherapy at Tonic Physio simply continues from the foundation built during prehab. This continuity improves outcomes and reduces the uncertainty that many patients feel after surgery.
