# Workplace Injury Physiotherapy: Returning Safely After a Work-Related Injury

Workplace injuries are one of the leading causes of disability and lost productivity. Whether you work in an office, a warehouse, on a construction site, or in healthcare, the physical demands of your job can lead to injury. At Tonic Physio in Milton, our workplace injury physiotherapy program helps injured workers recover fully and return to their jobs safely. We also work with employers to prevent injuries and support the transition back to work.

## Common Workplace Injuries

Workplace injuries fall into two broad categories. Traumatic injuries occur from sudden events such as falls, lifting accidents, vehicle collisions, and machinery incidents. Overuse injuries develop gradually from repetitive activity, awkward postures, prolonged sitting, and heavy physical labor.

Common conditions treated at Tonic Physio include lower back strain from lifting and bending, shoulder rotator cuff injuries from overhead work, knee injuries from kneeling, twisting, or falling, neck and upper back pain from desk work, carpal tunnel syndrome and repetitive strain from typing and manual tasks, slips and falls causing sprains or fractures, and whiplash from vehicle-related workplace accidents.

## The WSIB Process at Tonic Physio

The Workplace Safety and Insurance Board covers medical treatment and rehabilitation for workers injured on the job. At Tonic Physio, we guide you through the process.

We help you understand your rights and responsibilities under WSIB. We complete and submit the required forms to your employer and WSIB. We communicate with your case manager to confirm authorization. We provide treatment according to WSIB protocols. We report your progress and functional status at regular intervals. We work with your employer and WSIB to coordinate safe and early return to work.

Our goal is to restore your function so you can return to your job without long-term limitations.

## Early Return to Work Programs

Research shows that early, safe return to work improves physical and mental recovery. At Tonic Physio, we design graduated return to work programs that allow you to resume your job with modified duties while you continue treatment.

These programs reduce the risk of re-injury by matching your current capacity to job demands. They also reduce financial stress by getting you back on the job faster.

## Functional Capacity Evaluations

Functional capacity evaluations assess your ability to perform specific work tasks. At Tonic Physio, we conduct these evaluations for employers, insurers, and legal cases. The assessment measures your strength, endurance, flexibility, and tolerance for bending, lifting, carrying, and repetitive tasks.

FCA results provide objective data used to determine readiness to return to work, need for modified duties, and any permanent limitations.

## Injury Prevention Programs

Tonic Physio partners with employers in Milton and surrounding areas to reduce injury risk. Our workplace wellness programs include ergonomic assessments of workstations and job tasks, manual handling training to teach safe lifting and movement, stretching and warm-up programs, employee wellness screenings, and return to work planning for existing cases.

These programs reduce claim costs, improve morale, and prevent the recurrence of injuries.

## What to Bring to Your First Appointment

For a workplace injury, bring your WSIB claim number if your claim has been filed, your employer name and contact information, information about how the injury occurred, and any medical reports or diagnostic imaging related to the injury.

## Direct Billing for Workplace Injury Claims

We bill WSIB directly for approved claims. If your claim is pending, we can discuss private payment options until authorization is received.
