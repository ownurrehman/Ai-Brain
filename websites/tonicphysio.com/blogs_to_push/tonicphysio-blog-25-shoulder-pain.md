# Shoulder Pain Physiotherapy: Rotator Cuff, Impingement, and Frozen Shoulder

The shoulder is the most mobile joint in the body, allowing you to reach overhead, behind your back, and across your body. This mobility comes at a cost. The shoulder is also one of the most unstable and commonly injured joints. Shoulder pain can make simple tasks like dressing, driving, and reaching impossible. At Tonic Physio in Milton, we treat all types of shoulder pain with evidence-based physiotherapy that restores movement, strength, and function.

## Common Causes of Shoulder Pain

Shoulder pain has many causes, and accurate diagnosis is critical for successful treatment.

Rotator cuff tendinopathy and tears affect the group of four muscles that stabilize the shoulder. These injuries cause pain with reaching, lifting, and lying on the affected side. Shoulder impingement occurs when soft tissue is compressed under the bony arch of the shoulder during overhead movement. It causes sharp pain with raising the arm. Frozen shoulder or adhesive capsulitis involves tightening and scarring of the shoulder capsule, leading to progressive loss of movement in all directions.

Labral tears involve the cartilage ring that deepens the shoulder socket. They cause deep pain, catching, and instability. Shoulder bursitis is inflammation of the fluid-filled sac that cushions the rotator cuff under the acromion. It causes pain at night and with overhead activity. Shoulder osteoarthritis causes deep ache and stiffness in older adults, particularly those with a history of trauma or repetitive use. Muscle imbalance between the upper back, shoulder blade, and rotator cuff muscles creates abnormal movement and chronic overuse.

At Tonic Physio, our clinical examination identifies the primary cause and any secondary contributors.

## The Tonic Physio Shoulder Assessment

Your shoulder assessment includes a detailed history including onset, mechanism, and previous injuries. We assess posture and shoulder blade position. We test active and passive range of motion. We perform special tests to distinguish rotator cuff from labral, capsular, and joint involvement. We assess muscle strength of the rotator cuff, deltoid, and scapular stabilizers. We evaluate movement patterns during reaching and lifting.

If your symptoms suggest a structural tear or advanced arthritis, we refer for medical imaging.

## Physiotherapy Treatment for Shoulder Pain

Treatment at Tonic Physio depends on the diagnosis.

For rotator cuff conditions, we use manual therapy to restore joint mobility, strengthening for the rotator cuff and scapular muscles, and movement retraining to correct impingement mechanics. For frozen shoulder, we use progressive mobilization to stretch the capsule, home stretching programs, and sometimes corticosteroid coordination. For labral tears, we stabilize the shoulder with rotator cuff and shoulder blade strengthening while protecting painful ranges. For bursitis and impingement, we improve posture, restore shoulder blade control, and strengthen the rotator cuff to create space under the acromion.

We also employ pain management tools including ice, heat, TENS, and soft tissue release. Exercise progressions move from basic activation to functional and sport-specific demands.

## Recovery Timeline for Shoulder Conditions

Acute rotator cuff strain improves in four to eight weeks. Chronic tendinopathy may require eight to sixteen weeks. Frozen shoulder progresses through freezing, frozen, and thawing phases. Full recovery takes twelve to twenty-four months. Labral tears treated conservatively take eight to sixteen weeks. Post-surgical labral repair requires six to twelve months of structured rehabilitation. Post-operative rotator cuff repair requires six to twelve months with strict protocols to protect the tendon.

## When Surgery Is Considered

Surgery is considered when there is a full-thickness rotator cuff tear with weakness and failed conservative care, recurrent shoulder dislocation with labral damage, or advanced arthritis with severe pain not responsive to rehabilitation. At Tonic Physio, we exhaust conservative options first and coordinate seamlessly with orthopedic surgeons when surgery is necessary.
