## 🔴 Critical Issues (Fix Before Publishing)

### 1. No TL;DR / Direct Answer Block
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Impact:** AEO extraction failure — Google/AI engines cannot extract a concise answer
- **Fix:** Add a 2–3 sentence summary immediately after the H1 that answers "How do you recover from back pain in Milton?"

### 2. Massive Wall-of-Text Paragraphs
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Impact:** Average paragraph = 4–6 sentences, some exceed 8. Mobile users bounce. Google measures dwell time.
- **Fix:** Break every paragraph to 2–3 sentences max. Use line breaks liberally.

### 3. No Bullet or Numbered Lists in Body
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Impact:** Zero scannable formatting. Competitors use lists heavily. Google's NLP favors list extraction.
- **Fix:** Convert all multi-item explanations (steps, symptoms, treatments) into bullet or numbered lists.

### 4. No Comparison Table
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Impact:** "Physio vs chiro vs osteo" section is prose-only. Tables rank in featured snippets.
- **Fix:** Convert the comparison section into a formatted table.

### 5. Keyword Cannibalization Risk
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Impact:** Title targets "back pain recovery" but existing pages target:
  - `/physiotherapy-for-lower-back-pain/` — "lower back pain"
  - `/best-physiotherapy-exercises-lower-back-pain/` — "best exercises lower back pain"
  - `/physiotherapy-for-sciatica-and-nerve-pain/` — "sciatica nerve pain"
- **Fix:** Narrow this post to a unique angle: "Back Pain Recovery Timeline & Treatment Stages" or merge/redirect overlapping posts.

### 6. No Table of Contents
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Impact:** 2,400+ word post with no jump links. Poor UX, lower CTR in SERPs.
- **Fix:** Add clickable TOC with anchor links to all H2s.

### 7. Thin Internal Linking
- **Severity:** Critical
- **Impact:** Only 4 internal links in a 2,400-word article. Competitors link 8–12+ times.
- **Fix:** Add contextual links to related service and blog pages (see mapping in rewrite).
