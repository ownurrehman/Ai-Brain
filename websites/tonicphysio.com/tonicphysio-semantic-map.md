## 🏗️ The 4 Revenue Pillars (Service Clusters)

### Pillar 1: Advanced Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation
**Primary Goal:** Restore function, manage acute trauma, and prevent chronic relapse.
- **Target Entities:** MVA, WSIB, Post-Surgical Rehab, Core Stability, Range of Motion, Neuromuscular Re-education.
- **Existing Assets:**

    - *Stop Managing Back Pain* (ID 12725)
- **Semantic Frames to Cover:**

    - **Comparison:** Physio vs. Home Exercise (Why professional loading is safer).

- **Conversion Target:** `/physiotherapy-in-milton/`

### Pillar 2: Therapeutic & Sports Massage
**Primary Goal:** Resolve soft tissue restrictions, reduce systemic tension, and accelerate muscle recovery.
- **Target Entities:** Myofascial Release, Trigger Points, Cortisol Reduction, Lactic Acid, Parasympathetic Activation.
- **Semantic Frames to Cover:**
    - **Benefits:** Why massage is a prerequisite for successful physiotherapy.
    - **Comparison:** Deep Tissue vs. Relaxation Massage.
    - **Process:** How myofascial release breaks the pain-tension cycle.
- **Conversion Target:** `/registered-massage-therapy/`

### Pillar 3: Shockwave Therapy (The Chronic Logic)
**Primary Goal:** Break through "plateaus" and resolve chronic, non-healing tendon issues.
- **Target Entities:** Tendonosis, Calcification, Acoustic Waves, Neovascularization, Chronic Inflammation.
- **Existing Assets:**
    - *Golfer's Elbow Guide* (ID 7735)
- **Semantic Frames to Cover:**
    - **Logic:** Why "rest" fails for chronic tendons (The need for controlled micro-trauma).
    - **Tools:** Shockwave vs. Cortisone injections.
    - **Case Study:** Resolving "frozen" joints or long-term heel pain.
- **Conversion Target:** `/shockwave-therapy/`

### Pillar 4: Compression Therapy & Circulation
**Primary Goal:** Manage edema, improve venous return, and support joint stability.
- **Target Entities:** Venous Insufficiency, Lymphedema, Peripheral Edema, mmHg Pressure, Venous Return.
- **Semantic Frames to Cover:**
    - **Components:** The science of graduated compression.
    - **Implementation:** When to wear sleeves vs. socks.
    - **Comparison:** Compression vs. Manual Lymphatic Drainage.
- **Conversion Target:** `/compression-therapy/` (and related product pages)
