# Coinsfera.com SEO Audit + Recovery Strategy
**Date:** 2026-05-14
**Client:** Coinsfera (Crypto OTC Exchange, Istanbul, Turkey)
**Status:** Dropped from #1 to #3 — Recovery Mode

---

## Executive Summary

Coinsfera dropped from #1 to #3 across primary Istanbul crypto keywords. The drop correlates with:
1. Competitors building dedicated landing pages per transaction type
2. Missing GBP / LocalBusiness schema
3. Thin content vs. competitors with 2,000+ word guides
4. No FAQ schema or rich snippets
5. Weak backlink diversity (relying on press releases)

## Why Coinsfera Dropped

### 1. Content Depth Gap
- **Coinsfera pages:** 400-600 words
- **Competitors:** 1,500-2,500 words with step-by-step guides, FAQs, security tips
- **Fix:** Expand each service page to 1,500+ words with:
  - Step-by-step process (with screenshots)
  - Safety tips
  - Fee structure table
  - Supported coins list
  - FAQ section (10+ questions)

### 2. Missing Local SEO Signals
- **Google Business Profile:** Not found in search results
- **LocalBusiness Schema:** Likely missing or incomplete
- **NAP Consistency:** Present on site but not across web
- **Reviews:** Trustpilot exists but GBP reviews missing
- **Fix:**
  - Create/claim GBP listing
  - Add LocalBusiness schema with geo coordinates
  - Get 10+ GBP reviews
  - Add coins to GBP services

### 3. Missing FAQ Schema
- Competitors showing rich snippets for:
  - "How to sell Bitcoin in Istanbul?"
  - "Is it legal to buy crypto in Turkey?"
  - "What ID do I need?"
- **Fix:** Add FAQ schema to every service page (10 Q&A minimum)

### 4. Thin Internal Linking
- Service pages link to homepage only
- No cross-linking between buy/sell pages
- No hub-and-spoke structure
- **Fix:** Create /istanbul/ hub page linking to all services

### 5. Weak Backlink Profile
- Press releases (newsfilecorp, barchart, globeandmail) = low authority
- No industry directory listings
- No local Turkish business directories
- **Fix:**
  - Submit to Turkish business directories
  - Get listed on coinatmradar.com
  - Crypto forum profiles (bitcointalk, reddit)
  - Local Istanbul business listings

### 6. No Video Content
- YouTube channel exists but not optimized
- No video schema
- No office walkthrough video
- **Fix:** Upload office tour, transaction process video

### 7. Technical Issues (Potential)
- Hreflang present but not verified for errors
- Image-heavy homepage (may slow LCP)
- No Core Web Vitals data available without testing
