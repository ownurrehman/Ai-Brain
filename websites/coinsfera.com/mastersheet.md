## Why We Dropped (Evidence-Based)

### Critical Issues
1. **Thin content** — 400-600 words vs competitor 1,500-2,500 words
2. **Stale content** — No blog updates since 2021 (4+ years old)
3. **No FAQ schema** — Missing snippet positions
4. **Stagnant backlinks** — No new links since 2021

### High-Impact Issues
5. **Weak internal linking** — No hub page, no cross-selling between buy/sell pages
6. **Missing transactional pages** — No "sell-bitcoin-to-cash" exact-match pages
7. **Weak social proof** — Only 5 Trustpilot reviews, no widget on site

### What Is NOT The Problem
- ❌ Speed (CWV passes: LCP 2.0s, INP 189ms, CLS 0.01)
- ❌ Crawlability (robots.txt good, sitemap valid, hreflang present)
- ❌ GBP missing (it exists with 4.9 rating)
