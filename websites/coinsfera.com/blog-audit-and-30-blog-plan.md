## 4. IMPLEMENTATION RULES

### Content Standards for Each Blog Post:
- 2,000+ words minimum
- No em-dashes, no double-dashes
- No H1 in body (only H2/H3)
- No FAQ sections, no "Conclusion" headings
- No raw markdown, no emojis
- All paragraphs under 60 words
- Primary keyword in first paragraph
- Yoast SEO title <= 60 chars with "Coinsfera" brand
- Yoast SEO description <= 160 chars with "Coinsfera" brand
- Featured image from Pexels (unique, topic-relevant, alt text set)
- Category: Blog (ID 361)
- 3-5 internal links to landing pages
- 2-3 internal links to other blog posts (cross-linking)
- 1 blog per day spacing (July 1-30, 2026)
- No duplicate internal links within the same post

### Cross-Linking Strategy:
- Every blog links to 2-3 relevant landing pages
- Every blog links to 2-3 other blogs in the same cluster
- Blogs in different clusters link when topically relevant
- This creates a content net that funnels authority to landing pages
