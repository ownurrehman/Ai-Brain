## Why The Drop Happened (Real Analysis)

### 1. Content Freshness (CRITICAL)
- Your blog posts are from 2021 (4+ years old)
- Google prioritizes fresh content for crypto queries
- Competitors publish weekly/monthly
- **Fix:** Publish 2-4 posts/month on:
  - Crypto regulations in Turkey
  - Bitcoin price analysis
  - How-to guides (updated 2026)
  - Turkish crypto tax updates

### 2. Content Depth Gap (CRITICAL)
- Your service pages: 400-600 words
- Bitbanker pages: 2,000+ words with step-by-step, screenshots, FAQ
- **Fix:** Expand each service page to 1,500+ words with:
  - Before/during/after process
  - Fee tables
  - Safety tips
  - Comparison tables (OTC vs ATM vs Online)
  - 10+ FAQs per page

### 3. Missing FAQ Schema (HIGH)
- You have FAQ content but no schema markup
- Competitors get rich snippets
- **Fix:** Add FAQPage schema to every service page

### 4. Thin Internal Linking (HIGH)
- Service pages isolated
- No hub page for /istanbul/
- No cross-selling (sell Bitcoin page doesn't link to buy USDT)
- **Fix:** Build hub-and-spoke:
  - /istanbul/ → links to all services
  - Each service → links to related services
  - Footer → city-specific links

### 5. Backlink Stagnation (HIGH)
- Last press release: 2021 (newsfile, barchart, globeandmail)
- No new backlinks in 4+ years
- Competitors building weekly
- **Fix:**
  - Turkish crypto forums (bitcointalk Turkish section)
  - Istanbul business directories
  - Crypto conference listings
  - Guest posts on crypto blogs
  - Nominate for "best crypto exchange Istanbul" lists

### 6. Missing Transactional Pages (MEDIUM)
- No dedicated pages for:
  - "sell bitcoin to cash in Istanbul"
  - "buy bitcoin with cash in Istanbul"
  - "usdt to cash Istanbul"
  - "ethereum to cash Istanbul"
- **Fix:** Create 8-10 new landing pages for exact-match queries

### 7. GBP Optimization Gaps (MEDIUM)
- GBP exists but may lack:
  - Regular posts (weekly)
  - Photo updates
  - Service list (crypto exchange, Bitcoin, USDT, Ethereum)
  - Q&A section populated
  - Review response strategy
- **Fix:**
  - Post weekly GBP updates (market news, promotions)
  - Add 20+ photos of office, staff, transactions
  - Populate Q&A with common questions
  - Respond to all reviews within 24h

### 8. Missing Video Content (MEDIUM)
- YouTube channel exists but not linked to pages
- No video schema
- No "how to sell Bitcoin" tutorial
- **Fix:**
  - Office walkthrough video
  - "How to sell Bitcoin in Istanbul" tutorial
  - Customer testimonial videos
  - Add video schema to pages

### 9. Technical Issues (MEDIUM)
- Hreflang present (EN/RU/TR) but not verified for errors
- Image-heavy homepage (slow LCP possible)
- No Core Web Vitals data
- **Fix:** Run PageSpeed Insights, fix LCP/CLS issues

### 10. Social Proof Gap (MEDIUM)
- Trustpilot: 4.1/5 (good but not great)
- No Trustpilot widget on site
- No Google reviews count visible
- **Fix:**
  - Add Trustpilot widget to homepage

## Competitor Deep Dive

### BestChange.com (The Real Threat)
- **Why they rank:** 10,000+ pages, user-generated content, real-time rates
- **Their weakness:** No physical office, no local trust
- **How to beat them:** Emphasize physical location + instant service + no KYC delays

### Bitbanker.org (The Content King)
- **Why they rank:** 2,500+ word pages, schema, city hub pages
- **Their weakness:** No physical presence in Istanbul
- **How to beat them:** "Real office, real people, instant cash" messaging

### CoinATMRadar.com (The Directory)
- **Why they rank:** Massive database, location-based pages
- **Their weakness:** Lists competitors too
- **How to beat them:** Better LocalBusiness schema + more reviews

### Binance.tr (The Brand Giant)
- **Why they rank:** Domain authority, TRY trading pairs
- **Their weakness:** Online only, no cash, KYC delays
- **How to beat them:** "Cash today, no waiting, no limits"
