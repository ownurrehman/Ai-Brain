[All other pillars follow same hub-and-spoke model]

**Linking Rules:**
1. Every cluster post links UP to its pillar
2. Every pillar links DOWN to all clusters
3. Pillars link HORIZONTALLY to related pillars (e.g., GEO ↔ Semantic SEO)
