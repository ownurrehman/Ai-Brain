# Readability Guide for Enigma

## Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level Targets

| Audience | Target Grade | Avg Sentence Length |
|----------|--------------|---------------------|
| Beginner/Consumer | 6-8 | 15 words |
| General Business | 8-10 | 18 words |
| Technical/B2B | 10-12 | 20 words |
| Expert/Academic | 12-14 | 22 words |

## Sentence Length Guidelines

**Good:**
- "SEO helps your website rank higher."
- "Internal links connect related pages on your site."

**Too long (fix by splitting):**
- "SEO which stands for search engine optimization is a process that helps your website rank higher in the search engine results pages when people search for relevant keywords"

**Fixed:**
- "SEO (search engine optimization) helps your website rank higher. When people search for relevant keywords, well-optimized pages appear in search results."

## Paragraph Structure

**Maximum 2-3 sentences per paragraph.**

**Bad:**
```
SEO is important for any website that wants to get traffic from search engines like Google. When you optimize your content for relevant keywords, you make it easier for search engines to understand what your page is about, which can lead to better rankings and more organic traffic over time. However, it's important to remember that SEO is a long-term strategy and results don't happen overnight.
```

**Good:**
```
SEO is important for any website. It helps you get traffic from search engines like Google.

When you optimize for relevant keywords, search engines understand your content better. This leads to better rankings and more organic traffic.

Remember: SEO is a long-term strategy. Results don't happen overnight.
```

## Transition Words (Use Freely)

**For adding information:**
- Also, additionally, furthermore, moreover

**For contrast:**
- However, although, yet, whereas, conversely

**For examples:**
- For example, for instance, such as, like

**For results:**
- Therefore, thus, consequently, as a result

**For sequence:**
- First, second, then, next, finally

## Words to Avoid (Replace with Simple Alternatives)

| Instead of... | Use... |
|---------------|--------|
| Utilize | Use |
| Leverage | Use, harness |
| In order to | To |
| Due to the fact that | Because |
| At this point in time | Now |
| For the purpose of | To |
| In the event that | If |
| With regard to | About |
| Commence | Start, begin |
| Terminate | End, stop |

## Active vs. Passive Voice

**Prefer active voice:**
- "We optimize your website" (active)
- Not: "Your website is optimized by us" (passive)

**Quick check:** Can you add "by zombies" after the verb? If yes, it's probably passive.

## Bold Text Usage

**Bold 1-2 key takeaways per section.**

**Good:**
- "The ideal meta description is **under 160 characters**."
- "**Internal linking** is crucial for SEO success."

**Don't bold:** Entire sentences or paragraphs.

## List Formatting

**Use bullet lists when:**
- You have 3+ related items
- Order doesn't matter
- Quick scanning is helpful

**Use numbered lists when:**
- Sequence matters (steps, rankings)
- You reference items by number later

## Readability Quick Check

Before delivering content, verify:
1. Average sentence length < 20 words
2. Paragraphs are 2-3 sentences max
3. No passive voice unless intentional
4. Bold key takeaways present
5. Lists used for 3+ items
6. Transition words connect ideas
