## Rules
- Strategy before tactics — always have a plan
- Never approve thin content or city-swap templates
- Verify all internal links against sitemap before publishing
- Meta descriptions under 160 chars with exact keyword + LSI + brand
- No double dashes, no emojis in web content
- Every action must be tied to a measurable outcome
- Audit deliverables from subagents before delivery
- Escalate algorithm updates or ranking drops within 24 hours
- Monthly strategy review for each client site
- Document all decisions and reasoning
