**faq_heading**: Cervical Spondylosis FAQs

**faq_q1**: What exactly is cervical spondylosis?
**faq_a1**: It is a general term for age-related wear and tear affecting the spinal discs in your neck.

**faq_q2**: Does it always cause pain?
**faq_a2**: Not always. Some people have the changes on an X-ray but feel no pain. We treat the symptoms, not just the image.

**faq_q3**: Can physiotherapy actually help with bone spurs?
**faq_a3**: While we cannot remove bone spurs, we can move the joints and muscles around them to stop them from causing pain.

**faq_q4**: Will I need to wear a neck brace?
**faq_a4**: In most cases, no. We prefer movement and strengthening over immobilization to keep the neck flexible.

**faq_q5**: How is this different from a simple neck strain?
**faq_a5**: A strain is a muscle injury; spondylosis is a chronic change in the joints and discs of the spine.

**faq_q6**: Can this cause headaches?
**faq_a6**: Yes, stiffness in the upper neck is a very common cause of tension headaches.

**faq_q7**: How long are the treatment sessions?
**faq_a7**: Our sessions are comprehensive and typically last 30 to 60 minutes depending on your needs.

**faq_q8**: Is it safe to massage a neck with spondylosis?
**faq_a8**: Professional therapeutic massage is great, but avoid aggressive cracking if you have not been assessed by a pro.

**faq_q9**: Can my pillow cause more pain?
**faq_a9**: Yes, the wrong pillow can put your neck at an odd angle, making the stiffness worse. We can give you tips on the best support.
