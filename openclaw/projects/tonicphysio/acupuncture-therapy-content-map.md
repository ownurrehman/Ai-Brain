#### [H3] Common Acupuncture Questions
**FAQ 1: Does acupuncture hurt?**
A: Most people find it virtually painless. You may feel a slight tingle or a dull ache for a few seconds, but the needles are hair-thin and designed for comfort.

**FAQ 2: How many sessions will I need?**
A: Every body is different. Some feel relief after one visit, while others require a series of treatments for chronic issues. We will create a custom plan for you.

**FAQ 3: Is acupuncture safe?**
A: Yes. At Tonic Physio, we use sterile, single-use disposable needles and follow strict health and safety protocols to ensure your safety.

**FAQ 4: Can acupuncture help with anxiety?**
A: Absolutely. By calming the nervous system and reducing stress hormones, many patients find it helpful for managing anxiety and stress.

**FAQ 5: What should I wear to my appointment?**
A: Wear loose, comfortable clothing. Depending on the area being treated, you may need to roll up a sleeve or pant leg.

**FAQ 6: Will I feel sleepy after a session?**
A: It is very common to feel a sense of deep relaxation or slight drowsiness after treatment. We recommend drinking plenty of water afterward.

**FAQ 7: How is acupuncture different from dry needling?**
A: While both use needles, acupuncture focuses on the body's overall energy flow and systemic healing, whereas dry needling targets specific muscle knots (trigger points).

**FAQ 8: Can I combine acupuncture with physiotherapy?**
A: Yes, and we recommend it. Combining these therapies often leads to faster and more sustainable results.

**FAQ 9: Is acupuncture covered by insurance?**
A: Most extended health plans cover acupuncture. We recommend checking with your provider, and we can provide the necessary receipts for your claim.

**FAQ 10: How long does a typical session last?**
A: A typical session lasts between 30 to 60 minutes, depending on your specific needs and the treatment plan.
