**P2:** Explain osteoarthritis as the "wear and tear" of the joint. Use a simple analogy (like a door hinge losing its oil). Explain that when the cushioning wears down, the joint becomes irritated, leading to the ache and stiffness they feel.

**H2: How We Help You Move With Less Pain**

**P for H2 Second:** Explain that the goal is to stabilize the joint by strengthening the muscles around it, creating a "natural brace."

**Our Osteoarthritis Solutions (5 Points)**
- **Manual Therapy:** Hands-on techniques to improve joint gliding and reduce stiffness.
- **Strength Training:** Targeted exercises to support the joint and reduce the load on cartilage.

- **Activity Modification:** Helping you find ways to do your favorite hobbies without pain.
