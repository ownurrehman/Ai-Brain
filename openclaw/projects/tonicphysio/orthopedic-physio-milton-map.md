## FAQ Section

**Heading: Frequently Asked Questions About Orthopedic Physiotherapy**

1. **What exactly is orthopedic physiotherapy?**
   It is a type of physical therapy that focuses on problems with joints and muscles, such as sprains, fractures, or arthritis.

2. **Do I need a doctor's referral to visit Tonic Physio?**
   No, you can book an appointment directly with us, though we are happy to coordinate with your doctor.

3. **How long will it take before I feel better?**
   Every person is different, but many feel a difference after a few sessions. We will give you a clear timeline during your first visit.

4. **Will the treatment be painful?**
   While some exercises might be challenging, our goal is to reduce your pain. We always communicate and adjust the intensity to your comfort level.

5. **What should I wear to my appointment?**
   Please wear comfortable clothes that allow you to move easily, such as athletic wear or loose pants.

6. **How often do I need to come in for treatment?**
   This depends on your injury. Some people come once a week, while others may need more frequent visits initially.

7. **Can physiotherapy help with chronic arthritis?**
   Yes. While we cannot "cure" arthritis, we can help reduce the pain and keep your joints moving so you stay active.

8. **What is the difference between a massage and orthopedic physiotherapy?**
   Massage focuses on relaxing muscles, while orthopedic physiotherapy treats the underlying cause of pain to improve function and strength.

9. **Do you provide exercises I can do at home?**
   Absolutely. Home exercises are a huge part of getting better faster, and we provide clear instructions for them.

10. **How do I book an appointment at Tonic Physio in Milton?**
    You can book through our website or give us a call—we make the process quick and easy.
