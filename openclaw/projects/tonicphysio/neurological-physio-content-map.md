## FAQ Section

**Heading: Frequently Asked Questions**

1. **What is neurological physiotherapy?**
   It is a specialized type of physical therapy that helps people with problems in their brain or nervous system improve their movement and balance.

2. **Will this help me walk again after a stroke?**
   While every person is different, our goal is to help you regain as much mobility as possible through targeted exercises and support.

3. **How often do I need to come for treatment?**
   This depends on your goals and condition. We will work with you to find a schedule that fits your life and your recovery needs.

4. **Is neurological physiotherapy painful?**
   No. We focus on gentle, supportive movements. We always prioritize your comfort and safety.

5. **Can this help with the tremors associated with Parkinson's?**
   Yes. We use specific techniques to help manage stiffness and balance, which can help you move more confidently.

6. **How long does it take to see results?**
   Some people feel a difference quickly, while others see gradual improvement over months. We celebrate every small victory along the way.

7. **Do I need a doctor's referral to start?**
   In many cases, you don't, but we can help you coordinate with your doctor to ensure you have the best overall care.

8. **What should I wear to my appointment?**
   Please wear comfortable clothing that allows you to move easily, such as sweatpants and a t-shirt.

9. **Can my family members be involved in my therapy?**
   Absolutely. We encourage family involvement and can teach your loved ones how to support your exercises at home.

10. **Is this only for severe injuries?**
    Not at all. Whether you have a mild balance issue or a significant limitation, we are here to help you improve your daily life.
