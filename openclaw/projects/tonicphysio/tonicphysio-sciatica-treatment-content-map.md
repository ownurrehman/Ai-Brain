**faq_heading**: Sciatica Treatment FAQs

**faq_q1**: What causes sciatica?
**faq_a1**: It is usually caused by a herniated disc or a bone spur pressing on the sciatic nerve in the lower back.

**faq_q2**: Can sciatica go away on its own?
**faq_a2**: Sometimes it does, but without proper care, it often comes back or becomes a chronic problem.

**faq_q3**: Is it safe to stretch when I have sciatica?
**faq_a3**: Some stretches help, but others can make it worse. We show you exactly which movements are safe for your specific case.

**faq_q4**: How is sciatica different from a normal leg cramp?
**faq_a4**: Sciatica is nerve pain, which feels like an electric shock or burning, whereas a cramp is a muscle contraction.

**faq_q5**: How long does it take to feel better?
**faq_a5**: Many patients feel a reduction in intensity after a few sessions, though full recovery takes a bit more time.

**faq_q7**: Can sitting too much cause sciatica?
