## FAQ Section
**Heading: Common Questions About Pediatric Physiotherapy**

1. **Q: At what age should I start physiotherapy for my child?**
   A: There is no specific age. If you notice your child is struggling with a milestone or has difficulty with movement, it is always best to seek a professional assessment early.

2. **Q: Will my child find the sessions scary?**
   A: Not at all. We specialize in "stealth therapy," where we hide the hard work inside fun games and activities so your child barely knows they are exercising.

3. **Q: How long does a typical session last?**
   A: Sessions are tailored to your child's attention span and needs, typically ranging from 30 to 60 minutes.

4. **Q: Do I need to stay in the room during the session?**
   A: We encourage parents to be involved! Your presence provides comfort to your child and allows us to teach you helpful techniques.

5. **Q: How many sessions will my child need?**
   A: Every child is different. After the first assessment, we will provide a suggested plan based on your child's specific goals and progress.

6. **Q: Can physiotherapy help with "clumsiness"?**
   A: Yes. Many children struggle with proprioception (knowing where their body is in space). We use specific drills to improve balance and coordination.

7. **Q: What should my child wear to their appointment?**
   A: Comfortable, stretchy clothing like leggings or sweatpants and sneakers are best so they can move freely.

8. **Q: Do you work with other healthcare providers?**
   A: Yes, we believe in a team approach and are happy to coordinate care with your pediatrician or other specialists.

9. **Q: Is pediatric physiotherapy covered by insurance?**
   A: Most extended health plans cover physiotherapy. We recommend checking with your provider, and we can provide the necessary receipts for reimbursement.

10. **Q: What is the main goal of pediatric physiotherapy?**
    A: The ultimate goal is to give your child the physical confidence and strength they need to participate fully in school, sports, and play.
