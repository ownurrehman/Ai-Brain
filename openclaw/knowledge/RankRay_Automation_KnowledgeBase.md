## Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) - 2026 Insights
*Date: 2026-04-27*

### Core Concept
GEO is the practice of optimizing content to be cited by AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude). Unlike traditional SEO, which focuses on ranking (position #1), GEO focuses on **Frequency (Mention Rate)**—how often a brand appears across various AI responses.

### Key GEO Tactics for 2026
1. **Sub-Query Optimization (Fan-out):** AI breaks complex queries into smaller sub-queries. Content must rank for these shorter, fragmented queries to be retrieved via RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation).
2. **Direct Answer Architecture:**
   - Lead with the answer at the start of each section.
   - Use scannable formats: bullet points, numbered lists, and short paragraphs (2-3 sentences).
   - Avoid hiding content behind JavaScript, accordions, or paywalls.
3. **Authority Signals for AI:**
   - **Expert Quotes:** Use attributed quotes (Name, Title, Company).
   - **Citations:** Use named sources for statistics.
   - **E-E-A-T:** Prioritize first-hand experience and verified author credentials.
4. **Recency Bias:** AI citations drop sharply for content older than 3 months. Quarterly refreshes are mandatory.
5. **Off-Page AI Influence:** 
   - Unlinked brand mentions carry weight.
   - Focus on getting mentioned in sites AI already cites (e.g., Reddit, high-authority forums, Wikipedia).

### Actionable Strategy: "The AI Mention Loop"
- Identify pages currently cited by AI for target keywords.
- Insert brand mentions or expert contributions into those specific high-cite pages.
- This is the fastest way to trigger a "mention" in AI responses.
