## Lead Qualification

### Ideal Client Profile
- Revenue: $1M+ annually
- Employees: 10-100
- Location: UAE, UK, US, Canada
- Industry: Legal, medical, e-commerce, B2B services
- Current SEO: Either none or with underperforming agency
- Budget: $2,000-$10,000/mo

### Red Flags
- "We want results in 30 days"
- Budget under $1,000/mo
- No decision-maker access
- Previous 3+ agencies fired
- Unrealistic expectations
