## Competitor Keyword Insights (Industry Benchmarks)

Based on typical motorcycle gear retailer strategies:

### RevZilla Dominates:
- "Best [product type] for [use case]" comparisons
- Video review content (YouTube + embedded on site)
- Extremely detailed product descriptions (500+ words per product)

### Cycle Gear Strengths:
- Local SEO (physical store locations)
- In-store pickup keywords ("motorcycle gear near me")
- Loyalty program content (rewards, points, member deals)

### J&P Cycles Focus:
- Harley-Davidson specific gear (niche dominance)
- Cruiser/touring rider content (vs sportbike focus)
- Community/events content (rallies, group rides)

### Team Motorcycle Differentiation Opportunity:
- **Closeout/deal focus** — competitors don't emphasize discounts as heavily
- **Leather specialization** — stronger leather collection than most
- **Daytona local connection** — underutilized local SEO angle
