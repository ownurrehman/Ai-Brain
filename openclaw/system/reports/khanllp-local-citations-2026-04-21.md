## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Use consistent photos** across all citations (office exterior, team photos, logo)
2. **Encourage reviews** immediately after completing citations
3. **Monitor citations monthly** for changes or duplicates
4. **Remove duplicate listings** if found (especially on Yelp, YellowPages)
5. **Add services** to every citation that supports it
6. **Use UTM parameters** on website URLs to track citation traffic
7. **Respond to all reviews** (positive and negative) within 48 hours
