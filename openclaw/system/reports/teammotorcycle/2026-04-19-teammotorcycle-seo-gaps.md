## Executive Summary

TeamMotorcycle is a mid-tier Shopify-based motorcycle gear e-commerce store with ~400+ products across 60+ collections and ~20 blog posts. The site has a solid product taxonomy and reasonable collection-level content, but suffers from several significant SEO gaps: inconsistent meta titles, missing/weak schema markup, thin brand collection pages, anemic blog output, zero coverage for high-intent "parts & accessories" keywords, and typical Shopify performance bottlenecks. Below is a detailed breakdown.

## 5. Schema Markup & Structured Data Issues

### Homepage Schema Assessment
- **No visible JSON-LD schema detected** in the rendered homepage content (Shopify themes often auto-inject Organization schema, but the readability extraction shows no structured data markup).
- **Missing:** Organization schema (name, logo, URL, sameAs social profiles)
- **Missing:** WebSite schema with SearchAction (sitelinks search box)
- **Missing:** BreadcrumbList schema on collection/product pages

### Product Page Schema Assessment (Based on product URL analysis)
Product pages appear to use Shopify's default product schema, but potential issues include:
- ⚠️ Shopify auto-generates Product schema, but it often lacks `aggregateRating` and `review` properties for products without reviews
- ⚠️ No `offers.aggregateRating` on products with 0 reviews (many products likely have few/no reviews)
- ❌ **No FAQ schema** on product pages (missed rich snippet opportunity)
- ❌ **No BreadcrumbList schema** (collections → sub-collections → products)
- ❌ **No ItemList schema** on collection pages (missed carousel opportunity)
