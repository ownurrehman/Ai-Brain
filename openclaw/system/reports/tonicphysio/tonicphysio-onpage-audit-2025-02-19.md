## 3. Header Structure (H1-H6)

### Current Hierarchy
```
H1: Detected in hero section (likely implicit via Elementor)
H2: Multiple sections detected (services, testimonials, etc.)
H3: Service cards and subsections
```

**Issues Found:**
- **H1 is NOT clearly defined** - Elementor pages often have weak H1 structure
- **Multiple H2s without clear hierarchy** - "Our Services", "Why Choose Us", "Testimonials" all competing
- **Service page links in H2/H3** - Good for SEO if optimized

**Recommended Changes:**
1. **Add explicit H1:** `<h1>Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centre in Milton, ON</h1>`
2. **Structure H2s by priority:**
   - H2: Expert Physiotherapy Services in Milton
   - H2: Our Registered Massage Therapy Options
   - H2: Patient Success Stories
   - H2: Book Your Appointment Today

**Header Score:** 6/10
