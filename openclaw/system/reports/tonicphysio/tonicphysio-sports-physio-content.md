## 7. FAQs
**faq_heading:** Sports Physiotherapy Frequently Asked Questions
**faq_q_1:** How soon can I start sports physiotherapy after an injury?
**faq_a_1:** It is generally recommended to seek an assessment as soon as possible. Early intervention helps manage inflammation and prevents the development of compensatory patterns that can lead to further injury.
**faq_q_2:** Do I need a doctor's referral for sports physio in Milton?
**faq_a_2:** No, you do not need a referral to begin treatment at Tonic Physio. You can book directly with our specialists for an initial assessment.
**faq_q_3:** What is the difference between a general physio and a sports physio?
**faq_a_3:** While both treat injuries, sports physiotherapy focuses specifically on athletic performance, sport-specific biomechanics, and aggressive return-to-play timelines.
**faq_q_4:** Can you help me prevent injuries before they happen?
**faq_a_4:** Yes. We offer pre-habilitation services and movement screenings to identify weaknesses and implement corrective exercises to prevent future injuries.
**faq_q_5:** How many sessions will I need before I can return to my sport?
**faq_a_5:** Every recovery is unique. The timeline depends on the severity of the injury and your response to treatment, but we set clear milestones to track your progress.
**faq_q_6:** Will I need to wear special gear during my sessions?
**faq_a_6:** We recommend wearing comfortable athletic clothing and bringing the footwear you typically use for your sport to ensure accurate movement analysis.
**faq_q_7:** Do you treat amateur athletes or only professionals?
**faq_a_7:** We treat everyone from professional athletes to "weekend warriors" and active adults. Anyone who pushes their body physically can benefit from sports physiotherapy.
**faq_q_8:** What is a "Return-to-Play" protocol?
**faq_a_8:** It is a structured set of criteria and tests you must pass to ensure your body can safely handle the demands of your sport before you resume full activity.
**faq_q_9:** Can you help with chronic tendon pain that hasn't gone away?
**faq_a_9:** Yes. We use progressive loading and manual therapy to stimulate tendon healing and increase the load-bearing capacity of the tissue.
**faq_q_10:** Is sports physiotherapy covered by insurance?
**faq_a_10:** Most extended health insurance plans cover physiotherapy. We recommend checking with your provider, and we can provide the necessary documentation for your claims.
