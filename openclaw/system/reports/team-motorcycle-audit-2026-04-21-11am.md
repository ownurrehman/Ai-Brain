### 2. Motorcycle Jackets Collection (/collections/motorcycle-jackets)
- **Strength:** Has decent intro content (~500 words) with internal links

### 3. Bargain Bin / Closeouts (/collections/bargain-bin, /collections/motorcycle-closeouts)
- **Issue:** Duplicate content between Bargain Bin and Motorcycle Closeouts (both describe same concept)
- **SEO risk:** Cannibalization — two pages competing for "motorcycle gear deals" keywords
- **Fix:** 301 redirect /collections/motorcycle-closeouts to /collections/bargain-bin OR differentiate: Bargain Bin = discontinued sizes, Closeouts = last season models
- **Quick win:** Add urgency copy: "New deals added weekly" + email capture for deal alerts

### 4. Brand Pages (Alpinestars, Shoei, etc.)
- **Alpinestars page:** Shows "This collection is empty" — broken collection or out of stock
- **Shoei page:** Good brand content (~400 words) but no product listings visible in fetch
- **Issue:** Brand pages have content but likely no actual products linked (empty collections)
- **Fix:** Audit inventory sync — if products exist, fix collection filters; if discontinued, add "Similar Products" section
- **Quick win:** Add "Shop Similar Brands" cross-links on empty brand pages

### 5. Motorcycle Boots Collection (/collections/motorcycle-boots)
- **Issue:** Minimal content — only "Filter Gear By Category" visible (likely JavaScript-rendered products)
- **Fix:** Add 300+ word intro covering boot types (touring, sport, cruiser, adventure), safety features (ankle protection, oil-resistant soles), and sizing tips
- **Quick win:** Add H2 sections: "How Boots Protect You" + "Boot Materials Explained"

## 🔍 Technical SEO Gaps Identified

### Missing Schema Types
- **Product schema** on individual product pages (not fetched, but likely missing based on category patterns)
- **ProductGroup schema** on collection pages
- **Review schema** for products with customer reviews
- **Offer schema** for pricing and availability
- **BreadcrumbList schema** for navigation

### Content Thickness
- **Helmets:** <100 words (critical gap — highest commercial intent category)
- **Boots:** <100 words (critical gap)
- **Jackets:** ~500 words (acceptable, but could be stronger)
- **Gloves:** ~600 words (good)
- **Brand pages:** 300-400 words (good, but useless if collections are empty)

### Internal Linking
- **Strength:** Good cross-linking between related categories (jackets → pants → gloves → boots)
- **Gap:** No "Complete Your Gear" bundles or kit suggestions
- **Gap:** Blog content not integrated into category pages (e.g., helmet safety guides not linked from /collections/motorcycle-helmets)
