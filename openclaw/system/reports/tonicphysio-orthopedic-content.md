## 2. Main Service Description
Orthopedic physiotherapy focuses on the musculoskeletal system, targeting injuries and disorders affecting your joints, ligaments, tendons, and muscles. Whether you are recovering from a sudden sports injury, managing a chronic degenerative condition, or preparing for surgery, the goal is the same: restoring optimal function and reducing pain without relying solely on medication.

At Tonic Physio, we understand that pain in Milton residents often stems from a mix of high-impact athletics and the physical demands of daily commutes and desk work. Our orthopedic specialists don't just treat the symptoms; we analyze movement patterns to identify the root cause of your dysfunction. By combining manual therapy with targeted exercise, we help you regain strength and stability, preventing future relapses and improving your overall quality of life.

This service is ideal for those dealing with:
* Post-surgical rehabilitation (ACL, joint replacements)
* Chronic arthritis and joint stiffness
* Repetitive strain injuries
* Fractures and sprains
* Severe back or neck pain

## 4. Treatment Approach
**Step 1: Comprehensive Assessment**
Your journey begins with a detailed evaluation. We analyze your medical history, current pain levels, and physical limitations through movement screening and orthopedic testing to create a precise diagnosis.

**Step 2: Targeted Intervention**
Based on your assessment, we implement a blend of manual therapy, joint mobilization, and corrective exercises. This phase focuses on reducing acute pain and restoring the basic mechanics of the affected area.

**Step 3: Functional Restoration**
As you progress, we shift the focus toward strength and endurance. We introduce functional movements that mimic your daily activities, ensuring your body can handle the stresses of your specific lifestyle.

**Step 4: Maintenance and Prevention**
The final stage is about longevity. We provide you with a sustainable home exercise program and lifestyle adjustments to ensure your injury does not return.

## 5. FAQ Section
**What is the difference between general physiotherapy and orthopedic physiotherapy?**
While general physiotherapy covers a broad range of health issues, orthopedic physiotherapy specializes specifically in the musculoskeletal system. It focuses heavily on the bones, joints, and soft tissues to treat injuries and improve physical mobility.

**How many sessions will I need to see results?**
Recovery varies based on the severity of the injury and your overall health. Some patients feel relief after a few sessions, while chronic conditions or post-surgical rehab may require several months of consistent work. Your therapist will provide a projected timeline during your initial assessment.

**Do I need a doctor's referral for orthopedic physiotherapy in Milton?**
No, you do not need a referral to begin treatment at Tonic Physio. However, we are happy to coordinate with your primary care physician or orthopedic surgeon to ensure your recovery is integrated and safe.

**Is orthopedic physiotherapy painful?**
While some techniques like deep tissue mobilization or challenging exercises can be uncomfortable, treatment should never be unbearable. We communicate throughout every session to adjust the intensity to your tolerance level.

**Can physiotherapy help if I've already had joint replacement surgery?**
Yes. Post-operative rehabilitation is critical for the success of a joint replacement. We help you regain range of motion and build the supporting muscle strength necessary to make the most of your new joint.

**How long does a typical session last?**
Most sessions last between 45 and 60 minutes, allowing ample time for manual therapy and guided exercise.
