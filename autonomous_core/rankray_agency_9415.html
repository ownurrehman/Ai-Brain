What Is Your SEO Strategy?

Among the questions to ask an SEO agency before hiring them, this comes first. When it comes to SEO, having a solid strategy in order is important. You want an agency that takes a long-term, ethical approach – not one using shady “black hat” tactics that could get you penalized.

A good SEO strategy involves creating optimized content, promoting it through link building, and continuously analyzing and refining it based on results.

The goal?

To improve your visibility in search and drive more organic traffic to your site.

Key Components

Some must-have elements of an effective SEO strategy include:

  • Thorough keyword research to identify terms your target audience is searching for
  • Optimizing website content like pages, blog posts, and metadata with those keywords
  • Building high-quality backlinks through tactics like guest posting and outreach
  • Ensuring your site is fast, mobile-friendly, and technically sound

Playing The Long Game

SEO is a marathon, not a sprint. Successful agencies focus on sustainable white hat tactics rather than trying to “game” the system with quick fixes that won’t last long. When considering questions to ask an SEO agency, it’s essential to ensure they will help you create a content strategy centered around long-tail keywords and in-depth topic coverage.

The best SEO plans take a holistic view – utilizing technical SEO, on-page optimization, link building, local SEO (if relevant), and more – its more about continuously striving to create the best possible user experience.

What Results Can You Achieve?

A reputable SEO agency should be able to provide a strong track record of achieving measurable results for their clients. Ask to see case studies or examples of past successful SEO campaigns they’ve run. This will give you a good idea of the kinds of improvements you can realistically expect from them.

Realistic Timeframes

Ranking highly on search engines doesn’t happen overnight.

SEO is a long-term strategy that typically takes 4-12 months to really bear fruit. That said, when you have questions to ask an SEO agency, you can discover that a skilled agency can often achieve some “quick wins” within the first few months by optimizing your site’s technical foundation and targeting low-competition keywords.

Measurable & Achievable Goals

Any goals or projections an agency sets should be specific, measurable, and realistic based on your industry and current rankings.

Vague promises of “getting you to #1” are meaningless.

Instead, they should lay out a data-driven roadmap for steadily improving key metrics like rankings, traffic, and conversions over time.

Staying on Top of the Latest SEO Trends

Search engine algorithms are constantly evolving, so an effective SEO strategy today may be outdated in 6 months. When evaluating questions to ask an SEO agency, it’s crucial to determine whether they stay ahead of the curve on the latest algorithm updates, trends, and best practices to ensure the strategy produces sustainable long-term results.

How Do You Track and Report on Progress?

A reputable SEO agency should provide detailed reports and analytics regularly. This allows you to track the progress and ROI of their efforts.

Don’t settle for vague updates – demand comprehensive data and insights.

Monitoring Tools & Metrics

An experienced SEO partner will use industry tools to monitor key metrics like:

  • Google Analytics for website traffic, behavior, and conversions
  • Rank tracking tools to see daily keyword ranking movements
  • Site auditing tools to identify technical SEO issues

They should define a solid strategy for continually measuring and improving your online visibility.

Transparent Communication

Beyond just numbers, your SEO agency should explain what the data means in plain language. They should walk you through insights, recommendations, and the plan for continued optimization. Open communication keeps everyone aligned on the same page.

What Is Your Experience in My Industry?

Another question in the list of important questions to ask an SEO agency before hiring them is about their experience in your relevant field. An SEO agency with expertise in your specific area of interest is important for achieving the desired results. They should have a deep understanding of your business, your target audience, and the competitive landscape.

Industry Knowledge Matters

SEO strategies can vary widely depending on the industry. An agency experienced in working with businesses similar to yours will have valuable insights into:

  • The search intent and user behavior of your potential customers
  • Relevant keywords and phrases to target
  • Content topics that resonate with your audience
  • Technical SEO considerations specific to your niche

An experienced agency has worked on numerous campaigns across diverse clients and industries. This enables them to apply proven tactics while customizing the approach for your unique business needs.

A Seasoned, Specialized Team

The strength of an SEO agency lies in its team. Look for an agency with specialists who have polished their skills across multiple clients and projects in your field. A seasoned, niche-focused team can hit the ground running and drive efficient, high-impact results.

Don’t settle for a one-size-fits-all approach. Prioritize agencies with a strong portfolio demonstrating success in your industry.

Why should I hire you vs. another SEO agency?

When choosing an SEO agency, you gain access to a dedicated team of experts deeply immersed in search engine optimization. The team should possess in-depth knowledge of the latest trends, algorithm updates, and best practices across all areas of SEO.

The team must include technical SEO specialists, content strategists, link builders, data analysts, and more. Having all these specialized roles in-house allows for the execution of a truly comprehensive SEO strategy tailored to specific business needs.

An in-house hire or freelancer likely won’t have the same diverse and deep level of expertise across all SEO disciplines.

Scalable & Cost-Effective Solutions

Hiring an entire in-house SEO team with specialists across different areas can be extremely costly, especially for small businesses.

Working with an agency provides a much more cost-effective solution while still using enterprise-level skills and resources.

The agency can easily scale efforts up or down based on requirements and budgets, utilizing cutting-edge SEO tools and data providers that would be expensive for a business to purchase individually.

Proven Track Record & Transparency

The agency should have a long history of driving measurable results for clients through ethical, future-proof SEO strategies. When reviewing questions to ask an SEO agency, it’s important to ask about their reporting practices. Regular, transparent reports showing key metrics should be provided so clients can track progress and ROI effectively.

They should also be highly selective about who they work with to ensure full dedication to each client’s success. This level of personalized attention is hard to find at larger agencies that take on too many clients.

What SEO tools do you use?

An SEO agency worth its salt should use industry-leading tools to research, analyze data, and monitor results. The best SEO agency will utilize a suite of powerful platforms – not just a single tool.

Top Tools for Keyword Research

For in-depth keyword research, most top agencies rely on tools like:

  • Semrush – Get keyword metrics, analyze competitors, track rankings, and more.
  • Ahrefs – Robust keyword explorer with search volume, keyword difficulty scores, and more.
  • Rankability – Unique metrics score keywords based on their rankability and business value.

Optimizing Content for Search

To ensure content is fully optimized for search, tools like MarketMuse and Clearscope are essential. MarketMuse in particular uses AI to analyze topical coverage and provide optimization recommendations.

Tracking Rankings & Reporting

Finally, platforms like BrightEdge, Conductor, and Moz are vital for tracking rankings across major search engines, monitoring backlinks, and producing detailed reports for clients.

An agency should be highly familiar with using (and paying for) a diverse set of premium SEO tools. That’s the only way to execute comprehensive strategies and drive meaningful results in the competitive world.
