# Post-Surgical Physiotherapy: What to Expect After Joint Replacement

Joint replacement is a major surgery. Whether you are having a hip, knee, or shoulder replaced, the road to recovery does not end in the operating room. Post-surgical physiotherapy is where the return to function actually happens. At Tonic Physio in Milton, we provide structured post-operative physiotherapy for patients who have undergone joint replacement, ligament reconstruction, fracture fixation, and spinal surgery.

## Why Post-Surgical Physiotherapy Is Essential

Without rehabilitation after joint replacement, surgery alone will not restore function.

After total knee replacement, the knee quickly becomes stiff and the surrounding muscles weaken from disuse. Early movement, strengthening, and gait training restore walking ability and prevent long-term limitations. After total hip replacement, precautions are needed to protect the new joint from dislocation. Controlled strengthening and gradual loading rebuild the hip muscles that support the implant. After shoulder replacement, the rotator cuff and surrounding muscles must regain coordination to enable reaching, lifting, and functional use of the arm.

Physiotherapy also prevents common complications including deep vein thrombosis, muscle wasting, joint contracture, and persistent gait abnormalities.

## Post-Operative Treatment Phases

Post-surgical physiotherapy at Tonic Physio is divided into clear phases.

Phase one is the early protection phase in the first two weeks after surgery. The focus is on wound healing, swelling management, gentle range of motion within precautions, and basic mobility training. Phase two rebuilds strength, active range of motion, and walking quality over weeks two to six after surgery. Phase three restores functional activities, balance, and endurance in weeks six to twelve. Phase four returns the patient to leisure, work, and sport requirements over months three to six.

Each phase has specific goals, protocols, and discharge criteria.

## Communication with Your Surgeon

At Tonic Physio, we maintain close communication with your orthopedic surgeon throughout your rehabilitation. We receive surgical reports, confirm post-operative protocols, and report any concerns. If a complication arises, such as excessive swelling, wound issues, or suspected infection, we refer back immediately.

## Home Exercise Programs

The majority of recovery happens between physiotherapy sessions. We provide detailed home exercise programs that are progressed as your function improves. We give written instructions and video demonstrations when needed. Compliance with the home program is the single biggest predictor of outcome after most orthopedic surgeries.

## Equipment and Modalities

Post-surgical physiotherapy may involve equipment to accelerate recovery. Continuous passive motion machines restore knee range of motion after total knee replacement. Neuromuscular electrical stimulation prevents quadriceps shutdown after surgery. Walkers and canes are prescribed and adjusted for safe mobility. Hydrotherapy may be introduced once wounds have healed and water immersion is safe.

## Recovery Timelines After Joint Replacement

Total hip replacement patients are usually walking with a walker or crutches within days of surgery. By twelve weeks most patients walk without aids. Full recovery takes six to twelve months depending on fitness level and surgical complexity. Total knee replacement patients need twelve to sixteen weeks before normal walking is restored. Flexion gains continue over twelve months. Total shoulder replacement recovery is slower, often taking six to twelve months for meaningful function.
