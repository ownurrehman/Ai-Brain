# Custom Foot Orthotics: When You Need Them and What to Expect

Your feet are the foundation of your body. If they are not functioning correctly, pain and dysfunction can travel upward to affect your knees, hips, lower back, and even your neck. Custom foot orthotics are precisely fitted devices that sit inside your shoes and correct abnormal foot mechanics. At Tonic Physio in Milton, we provide custom orthotic assessments, casting, and fitting as part of our comprehensive foot and lower limb rehabilitation program.

## What Are Custom Foot Orthotics

Custom foot orthotics are medical devices made from a mold or digital scan of your feet. Unlike over-the-counter shoe inserts, they are manufactured to match the exact contours of your arch, heel, and forefoot. They are made from materials selected based on your weight, activity level, and specific condition.

Orthotics redistribute pressure, correct alignment, absorb shock, and support weakened or collapsed structures. They do not simply cushion your feet. They actively change how forces move through your body.

## Conditions That Benefit from Custom Orthotics

Custom orthotics are prescribed for a variety of conditions.

Plantar fasciitis is the most common reason patients seek orthotics. The device supports the arch and reduces stress on the plantar fascia, allowing chronic inflammation to settle. Flat feet and overprotation cause the foot to collapse inward, straining the arch, ankle, knee, and hip. Orthotics restore alignment and reduce compensatory stress. High arches and underpronation create poor shock absorption, leading to stress fractures, shin splints, and joint pain. Orthotics provide cushioning and improve pressure distribution.

Metatarsalgia causes pain under the ball of the foot. Orthotics with forefoot cushioning and metatarsal pads relieve this pressure. Bunions and hallux valgus deformities benefit from orthotics that reduce abnormal forces on the big toe joint. Achilles tendinopathy and calf strain improve when foot mechanics are corrected, reducing strain on the tendon. Knee pain including patellofemoral pain syndrome often originates from poor foot alignment. Correcting foot mechanics reduces rotation stress at the knee. Lower back pain and hip pain can be aggravated by altered gait and posture from foot dysfunction. Even arthritis of the foot and ankle benefits from orthotics that reduce joint load.

## The Orthotic Assessment at Tonic Physio

Your orthotic assessment includes a detailed history of your symptoms, current footwear review, weight-bearing and non-weight-bearing foot posture analysis, gait assessment using video or pressure mapping, lower limb biomechanical screening from feet to hips, and a physical examination of joints, muscles, and movement patterns.

This assessment determines whether orthotics are appropriate for your condition and what type of device will best address your needs.

## Casting and Manufacturing

At Tonic Physio, we use precise casting methods to capture the exact shape of your foot in its corrected position. We may use foam impression boxes, plaster casting, or digital scanning depending on the case. The mold is sent to a certified laboratory where your orthotics are manufactured using materials selected for your body weight, activity level, and condition.

Manufacturing typically takes two to three weeks.

## Fitting and Follow-Up

When your orthotics arrive, we schedule a fitting appointment. We check that the device fits properly in your shoes, verify comfort and pressure distribution, make any necessary adjustments, and provide a wearing schedule to allow your body to adapt gradually.

Break-in usually takes two to four weeks. We schedule a follow-up to assess how your body has responded and make any final modifications.

## Custom vs Over-the-Counter Inserts

Over-the-counter inserts can be helpful for minor cushioning and support needs, but they cannot correct biomechanical problems. Custom orthotics are built from your actual anatomy and designed to change how your feet and legs function. For persistent pain or structural issues, custom orthotics are the appropriate choice.
