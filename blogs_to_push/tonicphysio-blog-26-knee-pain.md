# Knee Pain Physiotherapy: From ACL Tears to Osteoarthritis

Knee pain is one of the most common complaints treated by physiotherapists. The knee joint absorbs enormous force during walking, running, squatting, and stair climbing. When something goes wrong, the resulting pain can severely limit daily life, work, and sport. At Tonic Physio in Milton, we diagnose and treat all types of knee pain using evidence-based techniques that restore movement, build strength, and prevent recurrence.

## Common Causes of Knee Pain

Knee pain has many possible origins. At Tonic Physio, accurate diagnosis is the first step in effective treatment.

Patellofemoral pain syndrome causes pain around and behind the kneecap. It is very common in runners, cyclists, and people who sit for long periods. ACL and ligament injuries occur during sports and cause immediate swelling and instability. Meniscus tears create pain, catching, and locking in the joint. Knee osteoarthritis causes stiffness, swelling, and deep ache that worsens with activity. Iliotibial band syndrome causes pain on the outside of the knee in runners and cyclists. Patellar tendinopathy or jumper's knee creates pain below the kneecap in jumping athletes. Bursitis causes localized swelling and tenderness over the front of the knee.

Our clinical assessment identifies the cause of your knee pain without relying on unnecessary imaging.

## The Tonic Physio Knee Assessment

Your knee assessment includes a detailed history covering how the pain started, aggravating activities, and previous injuries. We observe your walking pattern and alignment. We test knee range of motion and compare it to the other side. We perform special tests to assess ligament stability, meniscus integrity, and cartilage health. We evaluate muscle strength of the quadriceps, hamstrings, hip rotators, and calf. We assess movement quality during squatting, stair climbing, and single-leg balance.

## Treatment Approaches for Knee Pain

Treatment at Tonic Physio is tailored to the diagnosis and the individual.

For patellofemoral pain, we address the muscle imbalances that cause the kneecap to track incorrectly. Hip strengthening, quadriceps retraining, and movement education are central. For ligament injuries, we restore stability through progressive strengthening, balance training, and neuromuscular rehabilitation. ACL reconstruction patients follow a structured nine to twelve month protocol that protects the graft while rebuilding full function.

For meniscus tears and arthritis, we reduce joint stress through strength training, manual therapy, and activity modification. For tendinopathy, we use progressive loading through eccentric exercises and sometimes shockwave therapy to stimulate tendon remodeling. For bursitis, we reduce inflammation through activity modification, ice, and targeted exercises.

## ACL Rehabilitation at Tonic Physio

ACL rehabilitation is one of our specialties. Our program follows the protocols established by surgeons and current research.

Phase one addresses swelling, range of motion, and basic strength in the first two to four weeks. Phase two rebuilds strength, proprioception, and functional movement over weeks four to twelve. Phase three advances to running, cutting, and jumping between three and six months. Phase four involves return to sport testing and graduated exposure to full competition between nine and twelve months.

Return to sport is only cleared when strength, hop tests, and psychological readiness meet established criteria.

## Preventing Knee Injuries

Many knee injuries are preventable. At Tonic Physio, we offer ACL prevention and sports screening programs that identify movement patterns and strength deficits before they lead to injury. Simple programs including jump training, hip strengthening, and balance exercises reduce ACL injury risk by fifty to eighty percent.
