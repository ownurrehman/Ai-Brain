# Physiotherapy for Seniors: Staying Mobile and Independent in Milton

Aging brings changes to muscles, joints, and bones. Strength decreases, balance becomes less reliable, and recovery from injury takes longer. Many older adults accept these changes as inevitable and reduce their activities accordingly. At Tonic Physio in Milton, we believe that much of the decline associated with aging is preventable and reversible with the right physiotherapy program.

## How Aging Affects Movement

Several age-related changes affect the musculoskeletal system.

Sarcopenia refers to the gradual loss of muscle mass that occurs with aging. Adults lose approximately three to eight percent of muscle mass per decade after age thirty. Decreased bone density makes bones more fragile and increases fracture risk. Joint cartilage wears down, leading to stiffness and pain in commonly affected areas such as knees, hips, and hands. Proprioception and balance decline, increasing the risk of falls. Reaction time slows, making it harder to catch yourself when you lose balance. Recovery time increases, meaning injuries and illnesses take longer to heal.

While these changes are natural, they are not inevitable. Targeted physiotherapy can preserve strength, restore balance, and keep you doing the activities you enjoy.

## Falls Prevention

Falls are the leading cause of injury among older adults and are responsible for most hip fractures and head injuries. At Tonic Physio, falls prevention is a core focus of our senior physiotherapy program.

We assess balance using standardized testing. We evaluate lower limb strength, ankle flexibility, and walking pattern. We assess vision and blood pressure considerations that affect stability. We identify environmental hazards and recommend modifications. We design strengthening programs that target the hips, knees, and ankles. We provide balance training on varied surfaces and in different visual conditions. We prescribe walking aids when needed.

Our falls prevention program reduces fall risk significantly and provides peace of mind to older adults and their families.

## Managing Arthritis in Older Adults

Osteoarthritis is common in older adults. It causes pain, stiffness, and reduced mobility in the knees, hips, hands, and spine. At Tonic Physio, we do not tell arthritis patients to simply rest. Instead, we create exercise programs that maintain joint mobility, strengthen surrounding muscles, and reduce mechanical stress on the affected joints.

Staying active is one of the most effective ways to manage arthritis pain. Our programs are designed to be joint-friendly and build the strength needed for walking, stair climbing, and daily activities.

## After Hip and Knee Replacement

Joint replacement surgery is increasingly common in older adults. At Tonic Physio, we provide orthopedic rehabilitation after total hip and knee replacement. Our programs follow surgical protocols, restore range of motion and strength, retrain walking mechanics, and facilitate safe return to home and community activities.

Most seniors return to walking within weeks and resume their normal routines within months with proper support.

## Staying Active and Independent

The most important goal of senior physiotherapy is helping you remain independent. We focus on the functional tasks that matter most. These include walking and stair climbing, getting up from a chair or bed, reaching into cupboards, driving and community mobility, gardening, golfing, and recreational activities.

At Tonic Physio, every treatment plan is built around your personal goals.
