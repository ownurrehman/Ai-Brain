# Tonic Physio First Visit: What to Expect at Your Initial Appointment

If you have never been to physiotherapy before, you may be unsure what will happen during your first appointment. Understanding the process can reduce anxiety and help you prepare. At Tonic Physio in Milton, your first visit is designed to be thorough, informative, and comfortable.

## Your Arrival

When you arrive at our clinic, we will ask you to complete our intake form if you have not already done so online. This form collects information about your medical history, current medications, and the reason for your visit. We recommend arriving fifteen minutes early to complete this paperwork.

Please bring your health card, insurance card or insurance information, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical reports or imaging results such as X-rays or MRI reports. Comfortable clothing is important because we will need to access the area being assessed.

## Your Initial Assessment

Your physiotherapist will spend forty-five to sixty minutes with you. We begin by discussing your symptoms in detail. We want to know when the problem started, what aggravates or relieves it, and how it affects your daily activities.

Next, we perform a physical assessment. This may include observing your posture and movement patterns, testing range of motion, assessing muscle strength, performing special tests to pinpoint the source of the problem, evaluating functional tasks such as walking, squatting, or lifting, and palpating the affected area to identify tenderness or tissue changes.

Your assessment is designed to be thorough but comfortable. We explain everything we are doing and why.

## Your Diagnosis and Plan

After the assessment, your physiotherapist will explain our findings in simple language. We will tell you what we believe is causing your symptoms and what we can do to help. We then create a treatment plan tailored to your needs and goals.

Your plan includes your diagnosis, recommended treatment frequency, expected recovery timeline, specific goals for your care, home exercises to support your recovery, and any referrals to other providers if needed.

We encourage you to ask questions. The more you understand about your condition and treatment, the more actively you can participate in your recovery.

## Your First Treatment

If time allows, we may begin treatment during your first appointment. This could include manual therapy such as joint mobilization or soft tissue release, therapeutic modalities such as heat, ice, ultrasound, or electrical stimulation, taping or bracing to support injured tissue, and education about posture, ergonomics, and activity modification.

You will also receive your first set of home exercises. These are just as important as in-clinic treatment and are designed to complement what we do during your sessions.

## How Many Sessions Will You Need

The number of sessions depends on your condition severity, how long you have had the problem, your overall health, and how consistently you do your home exercises. Acute conditions may resolve in four to eight sessions. Chronic conditions may require twelve to twenty sessions. Post-surgical rehabilitation takes several months. We will give you an honest estimate based on our experience and reassess regularly.
