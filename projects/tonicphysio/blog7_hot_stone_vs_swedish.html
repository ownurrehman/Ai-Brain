What Is Hot Stone Massage

Hot stone massage is a specialty therapy that uses smooth, heated stones placed on specific points of the body. The warmth from the stones penetrates deep into muscle tissue, promoting relaxation, easing tension, and improving circulation.

During a session, your therapist may also use the stones as an extension of their hands to perform massage strokes. The heat allows muscles to relax more quickly, making it easier to address deeper tension without applying excessive pressure.

This therapy is ideal for individuals who enjoy warmth, struggle with chronic muscle tightness, or find traditional deep pressure uncomfortable.

What Is Swedish Massage

Swedish massage is the most common type of massage therapy. It uses long, flowing strokes, kneading, circular movements, and gentle tapping to relax muscles, improve circulation, and promote overall well-being.

The pressure in Swedish massage is typically lighter to moderate, making it an excellent choice for first-time massage clients, those seeking stress relief, or anyone who prefers a soothing, relaxing experience.

At Tonic Physio, our therapists customize each Swedish massage to your comfort level, ensuring you leave feeling refreshed and balanced.

Which Massage Should You Choose

The right choice depends on your personal preferences and what you hope to achieve from your session.

Choose hot stone massage if you enjoy the soothing effect of heat, want help with chronic muscle stiffness, or prefer a therapy that promotes deep relaxation without intense manual pressure.

Choose Swedish massage if you are new to massage, want a classic relaxation experience, or prefer a lighter touch with smooth, rhythmic movements.

If you are unsure, our therapists at Tonic Physio will discuss your needs and recommend the best option. You can also combine both therapies in a single session for a customized experience.

What to Expect During Your Session

Regardless of which massage you choose, your session at Tonic Physio will begin with a brief consultation. Your therapist will ask about any health conditions, areas of concern, and your pressure preferences.

For hot stone massage, the stones are heated in a professional warmer and tested before application. You will feel gentle warmth spreading through your muscles as the stones are placed and moved across your body.

For Swedish massage, you will lie on a comfortable table while your therapist uses oil or lotion to perform smooth, relaxing strokes. You can request lighter or firmer pressure at any time.

Both sessions typically last sixty to ninety minutes, and you should feel deeply relaxed afterward.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is hot stone massage safe for everyone

Hot stone massage is safe for most people, but it may not be suitable for individuals with certain skin conditions, diabetes, or cardiovascular issues. Always inform your therapist of any health concerns before your session.

Which massage is better for stress relief

Both are excellent for stress relief. Swedish massage is ideal if you prefer a gentle, calming experience, while hot stone massage offers deeper relaxation through the soothing effect of heat.

Can I request a combination of both massages

Yes, many clients enjoy a session that blends Swedish techniques with hot stone therapy. Ask your therapist about a customized combination treatment.

How often should I get a massage

For general wellness, once a month is common. If you are managing chronic tension or high stress, biweekly sessions may provide greater benefit.

What should I wear to my massage appointment

Wear comfortable clothing. You will undress to your comfort level and be professionally draped during the entire session.
