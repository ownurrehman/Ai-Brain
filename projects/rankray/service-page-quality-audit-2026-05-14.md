- **Builder:** Elementor (NOT ACF) — content lives in `_elementor_data` JSON (123KB)
- **Template:** Uses a single-page Elementor layout with Hero + Services + Cases + Why Us + CTA + FAQ
- **Section count:** 3 H2s + 9 H3s + rich FAQ with H4s

- **Images:** Portfolio carousel, case study graphics
- **Schema:** 2 JSON-LD blocks (SiteNavigation + BreadcrumbList)
- **CTA:** Complex multi-field form with dropdown, phone, name, email
- **Why it ranks well:** Visual breaks, case studies, trust signals, lead gen infrastructure

### What Local SEO has that ACF pages DON'T:
1. **Portfolio/case study section** — "Law Firm", "Real Estate Portal", "Ecommerce Store" success stories with metrics
2. **Visual breaks between major sections** — gradient backgrounds, image carousels
3. **Multi-step CTA form** — dropdowns for service selection, "How Did You Hear About Us?"
4. **H4 subsections** — FAQ uses H4s (question) + paragraphs (answer) nested under H3
5. **Social proof** — review schema, Trustpilot widget, client logos

> **Key insight:** ACF pages should NOT try to replicate Elementor visual effects. They should match the **CONTENT PATTERNS** — specifically: case study mentions, stronger CTAs, and longer FAQ/Why Us sections.
