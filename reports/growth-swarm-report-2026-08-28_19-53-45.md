## SECTION 2: THE #1 FLAW IN COMPETITOR AUDIT DELIVERABLES — AND THE RANKRAY FIX

**The flaw: Competitor audits are keyword inventories, not page-level revenue maps.**

The typical agency audit deliverable contains:
- A 500-row export of "keywords your competitors rank for"
- Domain Authority / DR comparison bars
- A traffic chart: "Competitor X gets 14,000 visits/month!"

What it **never** answers:
1. **Which URL** ranks for each keyword (domain-level aggregation hides whether it's a service page or a blog post)
2. **What intent** the ranking page satisfies (informational readers vs. "physio near me" bookers)
3. **What conversion asset** sits on that page (booking widget, click-to-call above the fold, direct-billing/insurance info)
4. **Which rankings produce patients** vs. vanity traffic

**Why this fails the client:** The clinic owner sees "competitor gets 14K visits" and demands blog volume. The agency obliges — 30 posts/month, traffic rises, bookings don't — and the client churns at month 5 blaming "SEO doesn't work." The audit's framing *caused* the churn.

**The RankRay fix — the "Service Page Revenue Map."** For each of the 10 priority services, a one-page grid:

| Priority Service | Target Query | Tonic's Current Rank | Competitor URL Holding Top 3 | Their Page's Conversion Elements | Gap to Beat Them | Est. Monthly Calls at Top 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

This flips the sales conversation from "we'll get you traffic" to "here are the 10 pages, here's who's blocking each one, here's exactly what their page has that yours doesn't, and here's the call volume each slot is worth." It's an audit built as a **closing document**, not a deliverable artifact — and it's structurally impossible for a blog-volume agency to replicate without abandoning their own model.

**Sales usage:** Open the Tonic Physio pitch with a live 3-row sample (e.g., "physiotherapy Milton," "sports injury clinic Milton," "direct billing physio Milton") pulled from the SERP in real time. Demonstrate the hook in the first 10 minutes instead of describing it.
