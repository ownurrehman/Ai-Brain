## GATE 1 — INDEXABILITY: "Can Google see it?"

**What we audit:**
- Google Search Console Page Indexing report, triaged exclusion-by-exclusion: crawled–not-indexed, discovered–not-crawled, soft 404s, duplicates without canonicals
- robots.txt cross-checked against meta robots and X-Robots-Tag headers — hunting accidental noindex directives on money pages
- Canonical integrity: self-referencing validation, cross-URL conflicts, parameter and pagination canonical collisions
- XML sitemap hygiene: only canonical, 200-status, indexable URLs; lastmod accuracy verified against actual content changes
- Orphan detection: a four-way join of crawl data, sitemap, GSC, and analytics — every page no internal link reaches
- Redirect mapping: chains longer than one hop, loops, 302s deployed where 301s were intended

**Thresholds we enforce:** 100% of service/money pages indexable and self-canonical. Sitemap error rate under 1%. Zero orphaned commercial pages.

**What it costs you when it fails:** Every noindex leak and orphaned page is inventory Google will never shelve. You are paying writers to produce content that is structurally invisible — the tonicphysio crawl found 17 service pages in exactly this state.

## GATE 2 — CRAWL BUDGET: "Does Google spend its visits where it matters?"

**What we audit:**
- Server log-file analysis: Googlebot hit distribution by template, directory, and URL pattern
- Wasted crawl ratio: the percentage of bot hits burned on parameter URLs, faceted navigation, pagination loops, tag archives, and internal search results
- Click depth: whether money pages sit within three clicks of the homepage
- Internal link equity: contextual inlinks pointing to service pages versus blog posts
- Faceted navigation control: robots directives, noindex/follow patterns, URL parameter handling
- Bot-response health: TTFB under crawler load, 5xx spikes during crawl windows

**Thresholds we enforce:** Wasted crawl ratio ≤20%. Every service page reachable in ≤3 clicks. Every service page carrying ≥8 contextual internal inlinks from supporting content.

**What it costs you when it fails:** Google allocates a finite crawl budget per domain. Every bot hit on a filtered faceted URL is a hit stolen from a page that sells. On large blogs, this is the silent reason fresh content takes weeks to index — or never does.

**What the founder receives:** The *Crawl Spend Map* — where Googlebot's attention goes today versus where it should go, with the specific robots and internal-linking changes that redirect it.

## GATE 3 — CORE WEB VITALS: "Does the experience hold once it arrives?"

**What we audit:**
- Field data first (CrUX, GSC CWV report), lab data second — diagnosed at the *template level*, never as site-wide averages
