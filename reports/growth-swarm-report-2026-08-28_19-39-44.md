## 2.2 Pillar Deep-Dives (Full On-Page Copy + Check Lists)

### 🟦 R — REACHABLE (Indexability)
> If Google can't index it, it doesn't exist. We reconcile three datasets most audits never compare side-by-side: a **fully rendered crawl**, **Google Search Console's Page Indexing report**, and your **XML sitemap**. The gaps between them are where B2B revenue hides.

**What RankRay checks:**
- Index coverage reconciliation: indexed vs. *Crawled – not indexed* vs. *Discovered – not crawled* vs. noindex-excluded
- Canonical integrity: self-referencing defaults, conflicting signals, canonical chains
- Robots directives across robots.txt, meta robots, and X-Robots-Tag (catches staging disallows left live after launch)
- JavaScript rendering parity: raw HTML vs. rendered DOM — is your content JS-only?
- Orphan detection: money pages in the sitemap with zero internal links
- Sitemap hygiene: only canonical, 200-status, recently modified URLs
- Soft 404s and redirect chains >1 hop

### 🟩 A — ACCESSIBLE (Crawl Budget)
> Googlebot has finite patience for your domain. On bloated B2B sites, **40–70% of crawl hits land on URLs that can never rank** — faceted parameters, tag archives, internal search results, redirect chains. That's crawl budget burned on pages that will never produce a demo request, while your new solution pages wait weeks to be discovered.

**What RankRay checks:**
- Log file analysis: Googlebot request distribution by directory, template, and status code
- **Crawl Waste %** = bot hits on non-canonical, redirected, or error URLs (RankRay's proprietary headline metric)
- Click depth: % of revenue pages buried >3 clicks from the homepage
- Index bloat ratio: indexed URLs vs. URLs that *should* rank
- Parameter/facet handling rules
- TTFB as a crawl-rate limiter
- Internal link equity flow from high-authority pages to money pages

### 🟨 N — NIMBLE (Core Web Vitals)
> Field data over lab excuses. We pull 28-day CrUX data at the 75th percentile for **every key template** — because B2B sites only have a handful that matter: home, solution pages, pricing, blog, and your demo-request flow. A 4-second LCP on your pricing page isn't a "performance opportunity." It's a conversion leak with a ranking penalty attached.

**What RankRay checks:**

| Metric | Good | Needs Work | Poor |
|---|---|---|---|
| LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) | ≤2.5s | 2.5–4.0s | >4.0s |
| INP (Interaction to Next Paint) | ≤200ms | 200–500ms | >500ms |
| CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) | ≤0.1 | 0.1–0.25 | >0.25 |

- **LCP sub-part breakdown:** TTFB → resource load delay → resource load time → element render delay (so devs fix the *actual* bottleneck, not a guess)
- **INP:** long tasks, main-thread blocking, third-party scripts (chat widgets, tag managers, A/B tools, heatmaps)
- **CLS:** unsized media, injected banners, font-swap shifts
- Template-level scoring, not site-wide averages that hide the bleeding page
