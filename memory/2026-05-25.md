**Findings:**

- **P0 — Static-routing crash:** `ServeStaticModule.exclude: ['/api/(.*)']` in `rankray-hq-backend/src/app.module.ts` is incompatible with `path-to-regexp@8.3.0` that ships in NestJS 11. Fix: change the pattern to `/api/{*splat}`.
