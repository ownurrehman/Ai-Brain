## 5. Critical Gaps Identified

1. **No dedicated "Agentic SEO" page:** While Rank Ray has blog posts mentioning agentic SEO (Canada-focused) and a GEO page, there is no standalone service page targeting "agentic SEO services." Competitors like AEOEngine and agenticseo.agency already have this. This is the single biggest missed opportunity.

2. **Weak local SEO positioning:** Despite being based in Rawalpindi, Rank Ray doesn't appear in top 10 for "SEO agency Rawalpindi." Location pages exist but aren't ranking.

3. **Blog-to-service page linking:** Blog posts exist on "agentic SEO" topics but may not internally link to a dedicated service landing page (since none exists).

4. **Geo-modifier gap:** Most service pages lack Pakistan-specific optimization. The site targets global markets but isn't capturing the lower-competition Pakistan market.

5. **No press/PR visibility:** Competitors like HikeMyTraffic use PR releases to gain mentions. Rank Ray has no visible press coverage in SERPs.
