## 7. Differentiation Strategy (How to Beat the Top 5)

1. **AI Search Visibility section** — Only BrightLocal covers AI factors in depth. Making this a full H2 section positions us as forward-looking for 2026.

2. **"Google Maps" in the title and body** — No competitor treats Google Maps ranking as a distinct, titled focus. The article title already has this advantage. Make it a recurring theme throughout.

3. **Voice Search integration** — Zero top pages have a voice search section despite growing adoption. Include as sub-section under AI/2026 trends.

4. **Whitespark 2026 data** — The 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors survey (Nov 2025) is the freshest data. Most guides reference older surveys. Cite these percentages prominently.

5. **Actionable checkpoints** — BrightLocal's checklist does this well but is gated behind PDF download. Backlinko's guide is comprehensive but reads as prose. Combine both: step-by-step guide with embedded checklist elements.

6. **Local Service Ads mention** — Only add if it makes sense for Rank Ray's audience, but it's a gap competitors miss entirely.

7. **Measurement/ROI focus** — Most guides are "how to rank" vs. "how to measure success." Adding a tracking section addresses the ROI question that business owners actually care about.
