## 2. SERP & Competitor Analysis

### Primary Keyword: "buy motorcycle gear online reviews"

| Position | Competitor | Strengths | Gap for TeamMotorcycle |
|----------|-----------|-----------|----------------------|
| #1 | **RevZilla** (revzilla.com) | HD video reviews, massive brand authority, free shipping | Team lacks video review content |
| #2 | **MotorcycleGear.com** | Legacy brand (New Enough), established community | No community reviews on product pages |
| #3 | **MCGearHub.com** | Dedicated review site, deep comparison content | Comparison guides are shallow (blog articles ~500-800 words) |
| #4 | **J&P Cycles** | Parts + gear combo, long-established | Team is apparel-only, no parts |
| #5 | **Cycle Gear** | Physical stores + online, house brands (Sedici, Bilt) | No house/exclusive brand |

### 3 Keyword/Title Gaps Identified

1. **"motorcycle gear reviews"** — Search returns RevZilla, MCGearHub. Team's blog articles are reviews but don't rank. Titles lack "review" keyword explicitly.
2. **"best motorcycle [category]"** — Many blog titles use "Best X for 2024" (outdated year). Search intent has shifted to 2025/2026. Example: `/blogs/guides/top-8-fullface-motorcycle-helmets-for-2024` — should be updated to current year.
3. **"closeout motorcycle gear"** — The actual collection page is at `/motorcycle-clearance` but branded links and homepage say "closeouts." SEO title mismatch between branded term and URL.

### Trustpilot Presence
- 4-star rating, 15 reviews on Trustpilot — positive but thin. Competitors have 100s-1000s of reviews.
