### H2: Platform-Specific GEO Content Tactics: ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude
**Koray Principle:** Micro-Semantics → Tailor content signals to each engine's unique processing logic.

- **H3: How to Optimize Content for ChatGPT and ChatGPT Search**
  - **Data points to include:**
    - ~5.0 domains cited per answer
    - 47.9% of citations from Wikipedia; 44% from first third of content
    - 3.2x more citations for content updated within 30 days
  - **Tactics:**
    - Section density: 120-180 words between headings (avg 4.6 citations)
    - 19+ statistical data points = 5.4 citations (vs 2.8 with minimal data)
    - Content over 2,900 words = 5.1 citations (vs 3.2 under 800 words)
    - Place answer in first 40-60 words of each section
    - FAQ schema + inline citations = ~40% higher selection weight
    - Build brand presence on Wikipedia, Reddit, G2/Trustpilot (3x citation lift)
    - Bing Webmaster Tools + sitemap submission

- **H3: How to Optimize Content for Perplexity AI**
  - **Data points to include:**
    - ~7.3 domains cited per answer (more generous)
    - 46.7% of citations from Reddit (Profound June 2025)
  - **Tactics:**
    - Recency is the highest-weighted signal after domain authority
    - Content published/updated within 6-18 months preferred
    - "Last Updated" timestamps + regular refresh cadence
    - Stack community signals: Reddit, niche forums, Quora
    - Answer-first structure with definitive, declarative statements
    - Visual content: ~70% of general queries cite visuals (charts, infographics, tables)
    - Link to sources Perplexity already considers authoritative in your vertical

- **H3: How to Optimize Content for Claude AI (Anthropic)**
  - **Data points to include:**
    - ClaudeBot: ~370M monthly requests; 35.17% image fetches
    - Massive context window (~200K tokens)
  - **Tactics:**
    - Write for reasoning and synthesis, not extraction
    - Logical flow: problem → mechanism → evidence → implication
    - Factual density over length: specific stats, named methodologies, defined terms
    - Long-form, structured content: pillar pages, technical guides, research-backed articles
    - Claude synthesizes and paraphrases — prioritize coherent, well-organized content
    - Entity clarity: consistent brand description across all web properties
    - Apple Safari integration upcoming → Claude as consumer discovery layer
    - Claude's character prioritizes verifiable, well-sourced information
