# Verification

## Before done

- Inspect the changed files.
- Confirm the change stayed scoped to the task.
- Run the most relevant tests, builds, or commands available.
- Verify affected routes, pages, commands, or publishing flows when relevant.

## Reporting rules

- Do not claim success without evidence.
- State what was verified and what was not verified.
- Call out residual risks, assumptions, and backward-compatibility concerns.
- If verification was blocked, say exactly why.
