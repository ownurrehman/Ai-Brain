# SEO Engineering

## Operating model

- Start with search intent before content generation.
- Treat topical clustering as a product and data problem, not only a writing problem.
- Build systems that help choose pages, priorities, links, and opportunities.
- Avoid reducing SEO strategy to "AI writes blogs."

## What strong SEO systems do

- detect ranking opportunities
- group terms into useful clusters
- track SERP features and competitor movement
- connect pages to intent and internal linking decisions
- support schema, metadata, and page-quality improvements
- help website owners decide what to build or improve next

## Website-scoped thinking

- Keep workflows scoped to a website, property, or project.
- Preserve the relationship between keywords, pages, clusters, links, and search outcomes.
- Distinguish sitewide issues from page-specific opportunities.

## Content and decision logic

- Content quality systems should care about structure, completeness, differentiation, and matching intent.
- Opportunity detection should connect evidence to action: update page, create cluster page, improve links, improve metadata, or watch competitors.
- Competitor gap analysis should compare intent coverage and page strength, not just keyword overlap counts.

## Implementation cues

- Keep response models friendly to dashboards and workflows that answer "what should happen next?"
- Preserve metadata, schema, and internal linking logic as first-class SEO concerns.
- Prefer outputs that help ranking decisions over outputs that merely generate text.
