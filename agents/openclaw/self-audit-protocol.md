## Self-Audit Protocol

### Introduction
The self-audit protocol is designed to ensure the accuracy and quality of the main agent's outputs. This protocol will be integrated into the main agent's workflow as a mandatory 'Audit Phase' for all high-value deliverables.

### Checklist
1. Review the output for factual accuracy and relevance to the task.
2. Check for any grammatical or spelling errors.
3. Verify that the output meets the requirements and specifications of the task.
4. Evaluate the output for clarity, coherence, and overall quality.
5. Identify any potential biases or areas for improvement.

### Audit Commands
The following commands will be used to execute the self-audit protocol:
1. `fact-check`: Verify the factual accuracy of the output.
2. `grammar-check`: Check for grammatical and spelling errors.
3. `relevance-check`: Ensure the output is relevant to the task.
4. `quality-check`: Evaluate the overall quality of the output.
5. `bias-check`: Identify any potential biases or areas for improvement.

### Implementation
The self-audit protocol will be implemented as a mandatory step in the main agent's workflow. The protocol will be executed automatically for all high-value deliverables, and the results will be reviewed and addressed as needed.
