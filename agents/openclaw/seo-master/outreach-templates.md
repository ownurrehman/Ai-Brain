# Outreach Templates (Personalized First)

Use these as base templates. Always personalize with real observations.

## 1) Cold Email - Local SEO Opportunity
Subject: Quick SEO win idea for {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

I reviewed {{website}} and noticed a few high-impact SEO gaps that are likely costing qualified leads in {{location}}.

Two quick examples:
- {{gap_1}}
- {{gap_2}}

I can send you a 7-day action plan focused on rankings + conversions (not vanity metrics).

Want me to share it?

Best,  
{{your_name}}  
Rank Ray

## 2) Follow-Up #1
Subject: Re: quick SEO win idea for {{company}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Following up in case this got buried.

If helpful, I can send a concise audit with:
- top 3 ranking opportunities
- one technical fix
- one content fix
- one internal linking fix

Worth sending?

Best,  
{{your_name}}

## 3) DM Style (Short Form)
Hi {{first_name}} - I checked {{website}} and found a couple of clear SEO opportunities for {{location}} searches.  
If useful, I can send a short action plan with priority fixes. Want it?

## 4) Proposal Hand-Off Message
Thanks {{first_name}} - based on your goals, I will send a focused SEO proposal with:
- technical fixes
- content roadmap
- local visibility plan
