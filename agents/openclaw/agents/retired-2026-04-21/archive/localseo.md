## GBP Optimization Checklist
1. Complete all business information fields
2. Add all relevant categories (primary + secondary)
3. Upload high-quality photos (exterior, interior, team, logo)
4. Write keyword-rich business description (750 chars)
5. Add all services with descriptions
6. Enable messaging
7. Set accurate business hours
8. Add attributes (wheelchair accessible, etc.)
9. Post weekly updates
10. Respond to all reviews within 24 hours
11. Answer all Q&A questions
12. Add booking URL if applicable

## Rules
- NAP must be 100% consistent across all citations (exact match)
- Never create duplicate GBP listings
- Never fake reviews or incentivize reviews against Google policy
- Every local page must have unique content (no city-swap)
- Schema must match physical location data
- Track local rankings separately from organic
- Respond to every review (positive and negative)
- Photos must be real business photos, not stock
- Monitor for duplicate listings monthly
