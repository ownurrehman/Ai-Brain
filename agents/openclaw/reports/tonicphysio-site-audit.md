## 3. Missing Content & Semantic Cluster Map

Based on competitor analysis (MexPhysio, Physio-ON, New Heights), here is the suggested semantic clustering for missing topics to bridge the authority gap.

### Cluster A: Specialized Rehab (High Intent)
*Current gap: Pre- and Post-Surgical care is underdeveloped.*
- **Topic 1: Pre-Surgical Rehabilitation** (Preparing the body for surgery to speed up recovery).
- **Topic 2: Post-Surgical Recovery Milestones** (What to expect after knee/hip replacement).
- **Topic 3: Post-Op Scar Tissue Management** (The role of manual therapy in surgical healing).
- **Topic 4: Ligament Tear Recovery** (ACL/MCL specific rehab paths).

### Cluster B: Neurological & Brain Health
*Current gap: Heavy focus on basic neuro; needs specific condition deep-dives.*
- **Topic 5: Stroke Recovery Phases** (Early intervention vs. long-term maintenance).
- **Topic 6: Parkinson’s Disease Mobility** (Specialized exercises for balance and gait).
- **Topic 7: Multiple Sclerosis (MS) Management** (Managing fatigue and spasticity through physio).
- **Topic 8: Concussion Recovery Timeline** (Step-by-step return-to-play/work protocol).

### Cluster C: Pelvic Health & Specialized Women's Care
*Current gap: Good start with B-Pulse; needs more clinical depth.*
- **Topic 9: Diastasis Recti Recovery** (Healing the abdominal gap after pregnancy).
- **Topic 10: Pelvic Organ Prolapse (POP) Management** (Non-surgical options and exercises).
- **Topic 11: Pelvic Floor Health for Men** (Prostate surgery recovery and bladder control).
- **Topic 12: Menopause & Bone Density** (Osteoporosis prevention through weight-bearing exercise).

### Cluster D: Performance & Sports Optimization
*Current gap: Transition from "injury" to "performance".*
- **Topic 13: Gait Analysis for Runners** (Correcting form to prevent overuse injuries).
- **Topic 14: Mobility for Golfers** (Improving rotation and reducing lower back strain).
- **Topic 15: Functional Training for Seniors** (Maintaining independence and preventing falls).
- **Topic 16: The Role of Proprioception in Injury Prevention** (Balance training for athletes).

### Cluster E: Chronic Pain & Holistic Management
*Current gap: Transition from "what is pain" to "how to live without it".*
- **Topic 17: Central Sensitization Explained** (Why chronic pain persists after tissue heals).
- **Topic 18: The Mind-Body Connection in Pain** (Integrating psychology with physical therapy).
- **Topic 19: Ergonomics for Remote Workers** (Preventing "Tech Neck" in Milton's home-office workforce).
- **Topic 20: Myofascial Release vs. Deep Tissue Massage** (Which is better for your specific pain?).
