# Content Writing

## Core standards

- Lead with the reader problem or goal.
- Prefer short, concrete sentences.
- Make each section answer a real question.
- Cut filler, hedging, and repeated setup.
- End sections with a takeaway, action, or transition.

## Strong content signals

- Specific examples.
- Concrete claims.
- Clear framing of who the advice is for.
- Useful next steps.
