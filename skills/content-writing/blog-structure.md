# Blog Structure

## Default shape

1. Clear promise in the title and introduction.
2. Fast answer or framing near the top.
3. Main sections ordered by reader need, not by writer preference.
4. Examples, proof, or steps in the middle.
5. Short conclusion with next action.

## Section rules

- Headings should preview value, not label a vague topic.
- Put the most decision-helpful material early.
- Use lists when steps or checks matter.
