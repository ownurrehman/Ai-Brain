# 📈 RankRay CRO & High-Conversion Copywriting

> **Frameworks for landing page conversion rate optimization and high-intent copy.**

---

## 🎯 1. Above-the-Fold (ATF) Value Hook Formula
Every high-converting landing page must answer 4 user questions in 5 seconds:
1. **What is it?** Clear category descriptor (e.g. *7-Day Forensic Technical SEO Audit*).
2. **Who is it for?** Explicit ICP callout (e.g. *Built for B2B SaaS & High-Growth eCommerce*).
3. **What is the measurable outcome?** (e.g. *Fix crawl bottlenecks and recover lost search revenue in 48 hours*).
4. **What is the single action to take?** (e.g. *[Claim Your Free 48-Hour Technical Audit]*).

---

## ⚖️ 2. Competitor Comparison Page Matrix (`/compare/`)

Structure comparison pages (`rankray-vs-[competitor]`) using this layout:
- **Headline:** *Looking for an alternative to [Competitor]? Here is what makes RankRay different.*
- **Feature Matrix:**
  - Audit Depth: *Line-by-line dev remediation vs generic automated PDF dumps.*
  - Speed: *7-Day Sprint delivery vs 3-month discovery retainers.*
  - Link Quality: *100% manual outreach to DR 40-85 real sites vs automated PBN networks.*
  - Pricing: *Transparent fixed pricing vs opaque monthly minimums.*
- **Conversion CTA:** *[Book a 15-Minute Technical Consultation]*
