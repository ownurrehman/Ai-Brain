# SERP Intelligence

## What to inspect

- Dominant result types: blog posts, category pages, tools, product pages, videos.
- Intent pattern: informational, commercial, navigational, transactional.
- Content angle: beginner, advanced, comparison, template, checklist, local.
- Evidence of freshness, brand authority, or original data.

## What to conclude

- What page type Google currently favors.
- What depth and format seem required.
- Whether the target topic deserves a new page, a rewrite, or no effort.

## Avoid

- Copying competitor structures blindly.
- Ignoring mixed-intent SERPs.
- Making strong ranking claims without current SERP evidence.
