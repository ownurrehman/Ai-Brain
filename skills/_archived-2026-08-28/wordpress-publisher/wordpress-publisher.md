# WordPress Publisher

## Prepare before entry

- Finalize title and slug intent.
- Confirm heading structure.
- Gather internal links, external links, and media.
- Check whether excerpt, category, tags, or featured image are required.

## Formatting rules

- Keep paragraph length readable.
- Use real heading levels, not bold text as fake headings.
- Remove stray formatting from copied source text.
- Check lists, quotes, tables, and embeds after paste.
