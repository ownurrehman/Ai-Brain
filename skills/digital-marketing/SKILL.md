# Rank Ray — Digital marketing

**Agency:** [Rank Ray](https://www.rankray.com) — full-funnel digital marketing for clients.

## Use when

- A client or internal task needs **channel mix**, campaign planning, or marketing execution across **more than one** of: SEO, paid, content, email/social touchpoints, launches.
- You are scoping a **retainer or project** (goals, audiences, budgets, timelines, reporting cadence).
- You need a **coherent narrative** from positioning through landing experience (without doing full product build in one pass).

## Avoid when

- The task is **only** technical SEO / SERP work → use **`../seo/SKILL.md`** or **`../seo-services/SKILL.md`**.
- The task is **only** paid ads ops → **`../paid-acquisition/SKILL.md`**.
- The task is **only** implementation in **RankRay-HQ** or a WP plugin → that repo’s **`AGENTS.md`** + engineering skills.

## Rank Ray delivery norms

- Anchor on **client goals and KPIs**; avoid vanity metrics unless the brief asks for them.
- Separate **strategy** from **tactics**; document assumptions where data is missing.
- Prefer **one primary CTA** per funnel stage; align creative, landing, and tracking.
- **Compliance:** ads platform policies, privacy claims, and regional rules—flag uncertainty early.

## Workflow (summary)

1. Confirm objective, audience, geography, budget band, and success metrics.
2. Map funnel stages and owned vs paid vs organic roles.
3. Produce a prioritized plan (quick wins vs bets) with measurement hooks.
4. Hand off deep execution to the right skill (SEO, paid, content, web) with a short brief.
