## 🐦 2. Twitter / X Thread Hook Rules
- **Tweet 1:** Bold claim + quantifiable outcome + "Here's the full teardown 🧵👇"
- **Tweets 2-5:** Bite-sized technical screenshots or clear before/after data points.
- **Final Tweet:** Link to full case study or service landing page.
