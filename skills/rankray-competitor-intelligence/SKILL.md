# 🛰️ RankRay Competitor Intelligence & Market Forensics

> **Techniques for identifying competitor vulnerabilities, SERP gaps, and market opportunities.**

---

## 🔍 1. Competitor Vulnerability Discovery
1. **The "Audit Theater" Gap:** Find competitors delivering 100-page generic automated audit PDFs without actionable code fixes. RankRay wins by delivering line-by-line developer remediation.
2. **Backlink Quality Teardown:** Check competitor backlinks for toxic blog comments, foreign language spam, and private blog networks (PBNs).
3. **Content Cannibalization Scrapes:** Map competitor sites that have multiple thin articles targeting the exact same keyword.

---

## 🎯 2. Bleeding-Site Discovery Protocol
- **Target Profile:** High-revenue B2B or eCommerce sites that suffered an algorithmic traffic decline of >25% in the last 6 months.
- **Outreach Angle:** Identify the exact technical cause (e.g. CWV INP failure or faceted navigation crawl trap) and present a 3-point recovery plan to their CMO/Founder.
