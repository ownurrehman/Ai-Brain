# Paid Acquisition Playbook

## Campaign design

- One campaign objective per campaign.
- Separate by funnel stage: awareness, consideration, conversion, retargeting.
- Use consistent UTM and naming conventions for analysis.

## Test matrix

- Audiences: 2-4 core segments.
- Offers: demo, trial, lead magnet.
- Creatives: pain-led, proof-led, comparison-led.
- Landing pages: at least 2 message variants.

## Budget control

- Allocate 70% proven, 20% growth tests, 10% experimental.
- Define hard stop-loss thresholds for CPL/CAC.
- Review spend pacing daily in week 1.

## Optimization cadence

- Daily: check delivery, tracking health, and spend anomalies.
- Weekly: prune poor ad sets, refresh creative, reallocate budget.
- Monthly: revisit channel mix and funnel assumptions.

## Quality checks

- Track lead quality by source, not just volume.
- Ensure CRM feedback loop reaches campaign decisions.
- Keep attribution assumptions explicit.
