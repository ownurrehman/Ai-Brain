Summary: This skill structures paid media work into measurable tests with clear budget control and funnel alignment.

# Purpose

Turn paid marketing goals into controlled campaign plans that generate qualified demand without wasting spend.

## Use when

- The task covers Google, Meta, LinkedIn, or multi-channel paid campaigns.
- You need offer, audience, funnel stage, and KPI alignment.
- The goal is pipeline, demos, signups, or qualified trials.

## Avoid when

- The task is organic growth strategy only.
- The goal is only website copy edits.
- No budget, target metric, or offer exists yet.

## Required inputs

- Offer and conversion action.
- Audience and market geography.
- Budget range and time window.
- Primary metric such as CPL, CAC, demo rate, or ROAS proxy.

## Workflow

1. Define funnel stage and conversion event.
2. Match channel to audience intent and buying cycle.
3. Build campaign structure with clear naming and budget caps.
4. Launch small tests by audience, creative, and landing page.
5. Evaluate weekly, cut losers, and scale proven combinations.

## Expected outputs

- Campaign architecture and test matrix.
- Creative and copy angles.
- Budget and bid guardrails.
- Reporting view with leading and lagging metrics.

## Checks before done

- Conversion tracking is defined before launch.
- Creative matches landing page promise.
- Budget allocation matches hypothesis confidence.

## Common failure modes

- Scaling before tracking quality is validated.
- Blending awareness and conversion goals in one campaign.
- Judging channel performance too early without enough signal.
