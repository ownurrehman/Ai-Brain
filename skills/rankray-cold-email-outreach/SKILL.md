# 💌 RankRay B2B Cold Email Outreach

> **Directives and templates for high-response outbound lead generation.**

---

## 🎯 1. Cold Outreach Rules & Constraints
- **Length:** Strictly **80–110 words** per email.
- **Format:** Plain text only. No HTML styling, no images, no tracking pixels.
- **Subject Lines:** 2–4 lowercase words (e.g. `quick question re: tonicphysio`, `milton traffic drop`).
- **Sender Persona:** Sign off as "Oliver" or "Sheikh" with no corporate fluff.
- **No Hard Pitch:** Offer a free diagnostic finding or concrete observation first.

---

## 📧 2. The 3-Step Sequence Architecture

### Step 1: The Diagnostic Hook (Day 1)
```text
Subject: quick question re: {{prospect_domain}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Was reviewing the technical setup on {{prospect_domain}} and noticed your Core Web Vitals are failing the new INP benchmark on mobile, which is likely suppressing your rankings for {{primary_keyword}}.

We recently fixed this exact issue for a similar brand and unlocked a 35% lift in organic search traffic within 60 days.

I put together a quick 3-point fix for your dev team. Want me to send it over, or should I leave you alone?

Best,
Oliver
```

### Step 2: The Proof Point (+3 Days)
```text
Subject: re: quick question re: {{prospect_domain}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Following up on my note from Tuesday. 

Here is a quick snapshot of what happened when we resolved the faceted crawl bloat for {{case_study_name}}: their indexed page efficiency doubled and lead volume grew +42% in Q2.

Happy to share the exact audit notes for {{prospect_domain}} if you'd find it useful.

Best,
Oliver
```

### Step 3: The Soft Close (+5 Days)
```text
Subject: closing the loop: {{prospect_domain}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Assuming your team is fully booked right now. 

If you ever want to review your organic search bottlenecks down the line, feel free to grab our checklist at rankray.com.

Wishing you great success with {{company_name}}.

Best,
Oliver
```
