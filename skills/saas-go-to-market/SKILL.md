Summary: This skill turns vague SaaS marketing direction into a focused GTM plan with clear messaging and channel priorities.

# Purpose

Define a practical go-to-market direction for a SaaS offer so teams can execute with aligned targeting, messaging, and next actions.

## Use when

- The task asks for SaaS positioning, ideal customer profile, offer narrative, or launch plan.
- Product and marketing teams need one consistent message map.
- Channel choices depend on segment fit and value proposition clarity.

## Avoid when

- The task is purely technical product implementation.
- The work is only SEO keyword analysis.
- The work is only ad platform setup or optimization.

## Required inputs

- Product and problem solved.
- Customer segment or market.
- Core value promise and desired business outcome.
- Constraints such as budget, timeline, or team size.

## Workflow

1. Define ICP and highest-pain use case.
2. Write positioning statement, value pillars, and proof points.
3. Build message hierarchy for homepage, sales, and campaigns.
4. Select initial channels by speed-to-learning and cost profile.
5. Produce a 30-60-90 day execution outline.

## Expected outputs

- Clear ICP and segment priority.
- Positioning and messaging framework.
- Initial channel plan with sequencing.
- Risks, assumptions, and validation checkpoints.

## Checks before done

- ICP is specific enough for targeting.
- Messaging differentiates from likely alternatives.
- Channel plan includes measurable first signals.

## Common failure modes

- Trying to target everyone at launch.
- Confusing features with buyer outcomes.
- Picking channels before message and audience are clear.
