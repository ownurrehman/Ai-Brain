# SaaS GTM Template

## 1) ICP Snapshot

- Company type:
- Team size:
- Trigger event:
- Current workaround:
- Buyer role:
- Economic buyer:

## 2) Positioning Draft

- For `[segment]` who need `[job-to-be-done]`,
- `[product]` is a `[category]` that `[main outcome]`.
- Unlike `[alternative]`, it `[key differentiation]`.

## 3) Message Hierarchy

- Headline: one outcome, one audience.
- Subhead: pain + promise.
- Pillars: 3 max, each with evidence.
- CTA: one primary action per page.

## 4) Channel Prioritization

- Fast learning channels first: founder-led outreach, partner intros, niche communities.
- Scalable channels second: SEO, paid search, lifecycle email, webinars.
- Keep one owner per channel.

## 5) 30-60-90

- 30 days: positioning tests and landing page message test.
- 60 days: first repeatable acquisition loop.
- 90 days: improve conversion and retention signals.

## 6) Success Signals

- Message-market fit interviews completed.
- Demo or trial conversion trend.
- CAC payback proxy by channel.
- Early retention signal (week 4 or month 1).
