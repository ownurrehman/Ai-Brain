# Rank Ray 30-Day Semantic SEO Blog Strategy

## Goal
Publish 1 highly-researched, semantic SEO blog post every day for 30 days. Target keywords Rank Ray can actually rank for, filling critical content gaps, and driving organic traffic to service pages.

## Content Pillars (Rotating Weekly)

| Week | Theme | Target Keywords | Service Tie-In |
|------|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| Week 1 | Local SEO Domination | Local SEO, GMB, citations, city pages | Local SEO Services |
| Week 2 | Industry-Specific SEO | Law firm SEO, dental SEO, HVAC SEO | Industry Pages |
| Week 3 | Technical SEO Mastery | Core Web Vitals, schema, speed | Technical SEO Services |
| Week 4 | AI Search & Future | GEO, AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity | GEO Services |

## Daily Production Workflow

### Morning (Research Phase - 30 min)
1. Check Google Trends for trending SEO topics
2. Analyze competitor blog posts (Backlinko, Ahrefs, Semrush)
3. Find 1 target keyword + 5 LSI keywords
4. Outline the post structure (H2s and H3s)

### Mid-Day (Writing Phase - 60 min)
1. Write 1,800-2,500 word semantic SEO post
2. Include: exact match keyword in title, first 100 words, 2 H2s
3. Add 3-5 internal links to service pages
4. Include 1 CTA box linking to related service
5. Meta description: <160 chars with KWD + LSI + Brand

### Evening (Publishing Phase - 15 min)
1. Upload to WordPress with proper formatting
2. Add featured image (SEO-optimized alt text)
3. Set category: SEO or Digital Marketing
4. Add tags: target keywords
5. Publish and submit to Google Search Console

## Writing Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. **No emojis**
2. **No double dashes** (use proper punctuation)
3. **Meta descriptions:** <160 chars, exact match KWD + LSI + "Rank Ray"
4. **Internal links:** Verified against sitemap, max 1 link per page per post
5. **Anchor text:** "Rank Ray" → homepage only. Other anchors: descriptive.
6. **Images:** Never rename/move existing images. Add new images with SEO alt text.
7. **H1:** Only one per post. Include exact match keyword near front.
8. **H2s:** Minimum 4, include LSI keywords naturally.
9. **First paragraph:** Must include exact match keyword in first 100 words.
10. **Conclusion CTA:** Always end with a service CTA linking to relevant service page.
